**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

Iran and Oman are reportedly finalising maps for an alternative shipping corridor, setting up two competing systems: the US pushing freedom-of-navigation transits, Iran imposing its own routes. Iran also unveiled a new ballistic missile (the Qassem Basir, claimed 1,200+ km range) and threatened "preemptive and asymmetric warfare" rather than a purely defensive posture, and warned South Korea and Canada against supporting US-led Hormuz security efforts. Separately, a Saudi Aramco facility in Jizan was reportedly attacked on Monday. Brent crude is trading around $97/barrel and WTI above $92, both up sharply as markets price in prolonged disruption risk. Dubai and Abu Dhabi airports remain open but are seeing rolling delays; Emirates has cancelled some Gulf-route flights and Air Arabia has suspended Kuwait and Bahrain services.

This is the first entry in this report's running Hormuz thread with an actual, named UAE cost channel rather than a standing geopolitical risk with no clear transmission mechanism: Gulf News flags upward pressure on shipping and marine insurance premiums directly, plus fuel-cost exposure from the oil price move.
