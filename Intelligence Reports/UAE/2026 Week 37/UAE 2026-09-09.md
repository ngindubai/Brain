## Story 1: Dubai residential prices fall 1.7% year-on-year in August — first annual decline since February 2021

**Source:** Khaleej Times, dated 7 September (Cavendish Maxwell data) | https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/property/dubai-real-estate-home-house-prices-fall-first-time-in-over-5-years
**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]], [[SEO Portfolio]]

Cavendish Maxwell data shows Dubai's average residential price fell to AED 1,636 per square foot in August, down 1.7% year-on-year and 1.3% over the trailing three months — the first annual price decline since February 2021, spanning five and a half years of continuous annual gains before this. Despite the price fall, activity held up: August residential sales reached AED 23.4bn across roughly 10,900 homes (14% fewer than July), with off-plan properties still 75% of volume, and year-to-date transaction value near AED 270bn — though that YTD figure for the first eight months is running about 24% below the same period last year. Cavendish Maxwell's Ronan Arthur frames it as the market "entering a more mature cycle," citing fewer new launches, regional uncertainty, and a broader normalisation in buyer behaviour rather than a demand collapse.

This is the number [[Comparison Network]]'s mortgage and property-adjacent products should treat as the real inflection point, not just another data point in the ongoing softening story already on file. A first-since-2021 annual decline is the kind of headline that changes buyer psychology (more people waiting for the bottom, more room to negotiate) even before it changes underlying fundamentals — worth watching whether search volume or lead quality on any mortgage/property-comparison product shifts over the next few weeks as this headline circulates locally.

## Story 3: CBUAE's broader Central Bank Law transition deadline lands 16 September — distinct from the insurance-law deadline already on file

**Source:** Norton Rose Fulbright, Ashurst (via search) | https://www.nortonrosefulbright.com/en/knowledge/publications/757ebc41/uae-ushers-in-new-era-of-financial-regulation-with-central-bank-law-overhaul
**Related vault notes:** none directly

The one-year transitional period for entities to reconcile their positions under the UAE's new Central Bank Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025, in force since September 2025) ends 16 September 2026 — one day after the separate insurance-sector consolidation deadline (Federal Law No. 6 of 2025, 15 September) already tracked in this report's open loops. The Central Bank Law overhaul is broader in scope: it brings virtual assets, DeFi protocols, stablecoins, tokenized real-world assets, decentralised exchanges, wallets and bridges under CBUAE's direct authority for the first time. CBUAE retains discretion to extend the transitional period if it judges that necessary.

Two separate but adjacent regulatory deadlines now land within 24 hours of each other (15-16 September) — worth treating as one compliance week rather than two separate items when checking whether this affects any Orwell-adjacent UAE entity, particularly given the new law's reach into crypto/DeFi activity that wasn't previously under CBUAE's remit at all.
