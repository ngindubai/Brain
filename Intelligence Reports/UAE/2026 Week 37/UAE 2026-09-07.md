## Story 1: IPS 2026 opens at Dubai World Trade Centre — 30,000 visitors, 300+ exhibitors, DLD as strategic partner

The 22nd edition of the International Property Show opened today and runs through 9 September at DWTC, held in strategic partnership with the Dubai Land Department. Worth watching over the next three days rather than reacting to today: IPS attendance and developer sentiment at this event is one of the more reliable read-throughs on whether the Q2 cooling is stabilising or deepening into Q3, which matters directly for [[Comparison Network]]'s mortgage and property-adjacent products. ## Story 2: Dubai property transactions hit Dh523bn for the first eight months of 2026 — already 57% of all of 2025

**Source:** DLD data via search, published 1 September | https://edwardsandtowers.com/dubai-real-estate-market-aed-523-billion-2026/
**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

Context for Story 1 rather than a standalone story: Dubai real estate transactions from January through August 2026 reached AED 523.44 billion across 148,564 deals — 57% of the full AED 919 billion recorded across all of 2025, meaning Dubai has cleared more than half of last year's total volume in eight months. Property sales made up AED 349.83bn of that (112,020 transactions), split roughly evenly between ready (AED 180.05bn) and off-plan (AED 169.77bn) sales.

Read against the Q2 quarter-on-quarter price softening already on file, the picture is: volume remains extremely high in absolute terms even as per-unit prices and rents ease — a market normalising off a hot base rather than one that's actually turning down. No new [[Comparison Network]] action; useful context to have on hand when IPS wraps.
