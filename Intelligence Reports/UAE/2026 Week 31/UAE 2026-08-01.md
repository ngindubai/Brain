## Story 1: Emirates, Etihad, Air Arabia and Qatar Airways cancel more Gulf routes as regional tensions persist

**Source:** Gulf News | https://gulfnews.com/business/aviation/flying-from-uae-emirates-etihad-and-air-arabia-issue-latest-flight-updates-and-cancellations-1.500627330
**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

A fresh batch of flight cancellations landed this morning across four Gulf carriers. Emirates cancelled its Kuwait rotation (EK853/855/857/859 outbound, EK854/856/858/860 return), its Bahrain rotation (EK835/837/839 outbound, EK836/838/840 return) and its Amman service (EK905/906). Etihad cancelled its Kuwait pair (EY653/EY654) and select Bahrain services. Air Arabia cancelled Abu Dhabi-Kuwait and Sharjah-Kuwait/Bahrain routes across multiple flight numbers. Qatar Airways has suspended Bahrain, Erbil and Kuwait services through 7 August. flydubai is the one major UAE carrier still operating a full schedule.

None of the coverage names a fresh trigger incident for today specifically — this reads as carriers extending existing caution around Gulf airspace rather than responding to a new attack. Dubai and Abu Dhabi's own airports continue operating without disruption; this is a regional-routes problem, not a hub problem. But the list of affected destinations (Kuwait, Bahrain, Amman, Erbil) is now wide enough that it is closer to a standing "reduced Gulf network" than a rolling one-off cancellation list, and Qatar Airways putting a hard date (7 August) on its suspension suggests carriers are now planning around the disruption rather than reacting to it day by day.

This is the same open thread flagged for four days running: regional risk is now a scheduling fact of life for Gulf aviation, not a shock. Worth continuing to track for any read-through into UAE outbound travel and cargo costs, but there is nothing here that changes the picture materially from yesterday.

## Story 2: August fuel prices take effect — confirmed 20-fils rise across all grades

**Source:** Gulf News | https://gulfnews.com/business/energy/uae-petrol-diesel-prices-for-august-2026-announced-1.500625679 | Khaleej Times | https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/energy/uae-petrol-diesel-prices-august-2026-announced
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]]

The UAE Fuel Price Committee's August decision — flagged as pending in yesterday's report — was published on Friday 31 July and took effect today. It landed on the higher end of the range: Super 98 rises to Dh3.60/litre (from Dh3.40), Special 95 to Dh3.49 (from Dh3.29), E-Plus 91 to Dh3.41 (from Dh3.21), and diesel to Dh3.80 (from Dh3.60) — a uniform 20-fils increase across every grade. The Committee's stated basis is monthly review against global energy markets; coverage attributes the rise to oil prices climbing roughly a fifth through July on the back of the regional risk premium (see Story 1's underlying cause).

This resolves yesterday's open loop cleanly on the "real jump" side of the scenario range flagged in Thursday's report, not the "modest rise" case. It is a direct, if small, operating-cost headwind for anything fleet-dependent in the portfolio.
