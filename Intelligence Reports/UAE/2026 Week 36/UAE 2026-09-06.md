## Story 2: Fitch affirms Qatar's 'AA' rating with stable outlook

**Source:** Reported via search (aawsat/Gulf Business coverage) | dated 5 September 2026
**Related vault notes:** none directly

Fitch Ratings affirmed Qatar's Long-Term Issuer Default Ratings at 'AA' with a stable outlook on Saturday, citing confidence in the economy's resilience despite Ras Laffan's LNG export facility remaining offline since a March missile strike (an ongoing hit to roughly 17% of Qatar's annual export capacity, per prior reporting). No direct UAE angle, but relevant context for the wider Gulf-economy picture this report tracks alongside the Hormuz situation: Qatar's credit standing holding steady despite a real, ongoing production hit is a data point on how much resilience rating agencies are pricing into Gulf sovereigns generally, UAE included.
