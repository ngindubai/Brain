## Story 2: Aramco and Maaden form mining JV covering ~10% of Saudi Arabia

**Source:** Gulf News | https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/aramco-maaden-plan-mining-jv-across-area-nearly-10-of-saudi-arabia-1.500645737
**Related vault notes:** none directly

Aramco and Maaden announced a joint venture (Maaden 51%, Aramco 49%, pending regulatory clearance) to explore roughly 182,000 sq km of Saudi Arabia's "Zone-4 Transition Zone" for copper, zinc, lead and rare earths — inputs for EVs and renewables. Aramco brings geological data and AI-driven target identification; Maaden brings mining expertise. Global copper market currently $250bn, projected past $400bn by 2035.

No direct Orwell read-through, but it's a genuine data point on how aggressively Gulf sovereign-adjacent capital is diversifying into critical minerals — filed as wider-Gulf-economy background, the kind of macro shift that eventually shows up in how much discretionary capital chases UAE property and comparison-network-adjacent sectors.
