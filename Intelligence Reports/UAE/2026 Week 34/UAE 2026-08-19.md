## Story 2: DLD-Backed Property Expo Heads to Hyderabad to Chase Indian Buyers

**Source:** PropertyNews.ae / Gulf News, via Times of India Group partnership | https://propertynews.ae/uae/dubai/uae-property-showcase-targets-indian-investors-in-hyderabad/

Announced today: the Dubai Land Department is backing a two-day "TOI Property Grand Expo 2026" at the JRC Conventions and Trade Fairs in Hyderabad on 31 October–1 November, jointly run with the Times of India Group and Global Edge Advertisements. Indians already account for 20–22% of Dubai property investment by value, running above AED 30 billion a year, and Dubai's H1 2026 sales are cited at AED 286 billion.

## Story 7: Fixed-Rate Mortgage Rush Continues Against a Hawkish Fed Backdrop

**Source:** Khaleej Times / market data aggregation | https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business

Not a single today-dated event but a live, actionable rate picture worth logging precisely because of the dirham peg mechanics: UAE lenders are currently quoting one-year fixed Islamic finance from 3.75% (Sharjah Islamic Bank), two-year conventional around 3.78–3.89% (Emirates NBD), and three-year fixed near 3.95%, with 3-month EIBOR at 3.91%. Three Fed officials dissented hawkishly on 29 July — the most hawkish split since 2016 — and September hike odds have moved above 57%, with the 30-year Treasury yield hitting a 19-year high. Because of the peg, whatever the Fed does in September lands in UAE borrowing costs in full, with no independent monetary buffer.

**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

This is the one number worth Gareth actually watching this week: if the Fed moves in September, UAE fixed-rate quotes reprice fast, and MortgageCompare's rate tables and any "lock in now" content need to be current on the day, not a week behind. Worth a standing reminder to check the September FOMC date against portfolio content refresh timing now, while there's runway.
