**Mortgages**
- Rates unchanged this week: lowest conventional 3.89% (Emirates NBD, two-year fixed, salary transfer), lowest Islamic 3.75% (Sharjah Islamic Bank, one-year fixed). Relevant to [[Comparison Network]] / MortgageCompare content.

**Banking / CBUAE**
- CBUAE overnight deposit facility base rate holds at 4.40%. No rate action expected before the 16 September FOMC read-through.
- UAE's five largest banks (FAB, Emirates NBD, ADCB, DIB, Mashreq — 79% of system assets) posted a combined AED 38.1bn (US$10.37bn) H1 2026 net profit, up 7.8% year-on-year. Net interest margins are under pressure as asset yields have slipped to 6.1% from 6.8% following 2025's rate cuts — a headwind worth tracking for MoneyCompare content angles.

**Insurance**
- CBUAE's Financial Stability Report 2025 (released 17 August) puts total written premiums at AED 74.8bn, up 14.9% year-on-year. The Federal Law No. 6 of 2025 transition period for the CBUAE's consolidated banking/insurance oversight framework runs to September 2026 — see open loops.

**Stocks**
- Markets closed for the holiday window; DFM opened today around 5,911 in early activity. ADX was shut Friday 28 August for Mawlid. No closing data available for a genuine like-for-like comparison — normal trading resumes Monday.
- Bitcoin crossed US$80,000 (three-month high) and gold hit a three-month high, both attributed to currency weakness and geopolitical risk premium — relevant backdrop for the [[Ventures]] GPU compute and property-opportunity watches rather than directly UAE.
