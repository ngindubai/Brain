## Story 1: Oil rises as Hormuz deal hopes clash with Iran's new conditions

**Source:** Gulf News | https://gulfnews.com/business/energy/oil-rises-as-hormuz-deal-hopes-clash-with-irans-demands-1.500635700

**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]], [[Priorities]]

Oil prices rose in early Asian trading today — WTI to $78.71/barrel (+0.68%), Brent to $84.28/barrel (+0.87%), Murban to $80.25/barrel (+0.91%) — on reports that Iran and Oman are "nearing a final agreement" on new shipping lanes through the Strait of Hormuz. The catch, as in every prior update on this thread, is Iran's conditions: Tehran has tied a full reopening to further concessions from Washington, including sanctions relief tied to the wider conflict. The modest size of today's price move reflects real scepticism rather than confidence — a signed paper agreement doesn't guarantee tanker traffic resumes immediately, U.S.-linked vessels aren't guaranteed access even under a deal, and insurance coverage for the route remains unresolved. Separately, Saudi Arabia continues ramping shipments through Hormuz regardless of the stalled formal deal — roughly 34 million barrels since 17 June — and cut its main Asian crude price again on 6 August as it leans into the traffic that is flowing.

**Signal for Orwell:** Updates the long-running Hormuz open loop rather than resolving it — worth continued daily watching rather than a fresh deep-dive, since the shape of the story (progress on paper, unresolved conditions in practice) hasn't changed in weeks. No direct portfolio action from today's move specifically.
