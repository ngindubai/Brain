## Story 1: Dubai to launch "Rent Now, Pay Later" with zero-interest instalments in September

**Source:** Gulf News | https://gulfnews.com/business/property/dubai-to-launch-rent-now-pay-later-service-with-zero-interest-instalments-1.500639254

**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

A new Dubai scheme launching in September will let tenants pay annual rent to landlords in up to 12 monthly instalments at zero interest, with a bank paying the landlord the full annual rent upfront and recovering it from the tenant over the year. This directly targets the UAE's long-standing single-cheque or few-cheque rent payment norm, which has historically forced tenants to find large lump sums upfront or take out personal loans to cover it.

**Signal for Orwell:** Directly relevant to [[Comparison Network]] — this is a new financial product category (rent financing) that MoneyCompare-style comparison content doesn't currently cover, and it will generate real search volume once live in September. Worth flagging as a content opportunity ahead of launch rather than after competitors are already ranking for it.
