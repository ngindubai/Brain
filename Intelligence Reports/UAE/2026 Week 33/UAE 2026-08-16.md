## Story 1: US–Iran Hormuz ceasefire MoU expires tomorrow with no deal in sight

**Source:** Web search aggregating The New Arab, Middle East Monitor, The National | https://www.newarab.com/news/sunday-deadline-mou-looms-amid-us-iran-standoff-over-hormuz, https://www.middleeastmonitor.com/20260812-us-iran-agree-to-extend-60-day-ceasefire-ahead-of-aug-17-deadline/

**Related vault notes:** [[UAE Market]], [[Comparison Network]]

The 60-day ceasefire window agreed under the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding — the framework that has kept a lid on the wider US-Iran conflict since mid-June — expires tomorrow, 17 August. Pakistan's Deputy PM Ishaq Dar and Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi have spent the past week shuttling between Washington and Tehran trying to broker an extension, but talks remain stalled over two sticking points: security guarantees and freedom of navigation through the Strait of Hormuz. Iran's newly appointed security chief has attached fresh conditions to any Hormuz reopening — an end to hostilities in Lebanon, Palestine, Yemen and Iraq, lifting the US counter-blockade on Iranian ports, sanctions relief, unfreezing Iranian assets abroad, and compensation for war damage. The strait has been under an effective Iranian blockade, with vessels required to seek permission and pay transit fees, since the US and Israel struck it in late February.

No new attack on an ADNOC vessel is reported today — the last confirmed incident remains Friday 14 August's, the third in a week, which the UAE and Kuwait both condemned. Oil closed Thursday at Brent $87.07/WTI $81.25 after a volatile week; no fresh print found today.

**Signal for Orwell:** No direct action item, but this is the sharpest near-term marker on the whole Hormuz thread — either the MoU gets extended tomorrow or the ceasefire framework that has held since June lapses.
