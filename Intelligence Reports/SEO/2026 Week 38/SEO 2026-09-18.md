## Story 1: Google ad tech antitrust remedies unsealed, ordered to open AdX/DFP to rivals within 60 days

**Source:** AdExchanger / Qz | https://www.adexchanger.com/platforms/the-court-just-unsealed-judge-brinkemas-remedies-decision-in-the-google-ad-tech-antitrust-case-heres-your-tldr/
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]], [[Comparison Network]]

Judge Leonie Brinkema's remedies decision in the DOJ's ad tech antitrust case against Google was unsealed today. She rejected the DOJ's demand to force a divestiture of AdX, opting instead for behavioural remedies: Google must build API integrations connecting AdX and DFP to Prebid (the open-source header-bidding framework it previously resisted joining), submit AdX bids into rival publisher ad servers on the same terms DFP gets, share real-time win/loss bid data with publishers, and stop AdWords favouring Google's own ad tech in bidding. A court-appointed technical monitor gets full systems and source-code access for six years (the DOJ had asked for fifteen). Google must submit a proposed final judgment by 2 October, with remedies taking effect 60 days after the order is formally signed; the scope is global, not US-only. Google is expected to appeal immediately once the order is signed.

This is the single most consequential story in today's sweep, and it isn't an SEO story in the traditional sense, it's an ad-market structure story with direct downstream effects on the tools and revenue mechanics every publisher-facing site depends on. For the SEO Portfolio and Comparison Network sites, none of which currently run programmatic display at meaningful scale, the direct read-through is limited today. But if Prebid connectivity and real-time bid-data sharing genuinely open up publisher-side yield competition over the next 6-12 months, that's worth revisiting once remedies actually take effect, since better publisher yield economics from header bidding could change the calculus on whether any portfolio site is worth monetising with display ads rather than lead-gen alone. Filed as a watch item, not an action item, until the order is signed and remedies land.

## Story 2: Google Ads adds product-value bidding controls; Pinterest adds a paid Search placement format

**Source:** Search Engine Land (web search) | via Search Engine Land's 17 September coverage
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]]

Google Ads launched a beta letting advertisers manually adjust product values within automated bidding, so Performance Max and Shopping campaigns can be steered toward higher-margin or higher-priority inventory rather than optimising purely on predicted conversion value. Separately, Pinterest introduced a new ad format letting brands bid for prominent placement directly within Pinterest Search results and pin close-ups, expanding its search-ads surface beyond the main feed.

Neither is urgent for the portfolio today, both are incremental platform features rather than ranking-relevant changes. Filed for completeness under the tools/data sweep; worth a look if any portfolio site runs Google Shopping or Pinterest ads with meaningfully varied margin across SKUs or service categories, but nothing here changes organic strategy.
