## Story 1: The UK volatility spike stays unresolved, and stays unofficial

**Source:** Search Engine Roundtable | https://www.seroundtable.com/google-update-42091.html (15 Sept report, checked again today for follow-up)
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]]

Checked today specifically for a resolution or confirmation, as flagged in yesterday's brief. There is none. Barry Schwartz's original 15 September post described a "huge spike in chatter," UK searchers reporting poor-quality and spam results reaching page one, and traffic disruptions timed sharply around 4pm that day, hitting both organic and Google Ads performance. As of today, three days on, Search Engine Roundtable has published nothing further on it, Search Engine Land hasn't picked it up, and Google's Search Status Dashboard carries no logged incident. Schwartz's own view at the time was that Google would likely never officially acknowledge it, which is the standard pattern for these unannounced ranking shake-ups: no confirmation, no denial, just a slow fade from the SERPs and the forums.

The practical read: if this was a genuine ranking event rather than noise, UK-facing sites would have felt it 15-17 September and any recovery would show in that window regardless of what Google says. Worth a quick look at Search Console performance data for the UK-heavy portfolio sites over those three days, but there's no wider algorithm story to write yet, and forcing one would mean inventing content that isn't there. This drops to a straight open-loop watch below.
