## Two Friday items worth flagging early

**Google guidance: don't include fake or undisclosed incentivized reviews in review snippet structured data** (Search Engine Land, 24 July 2026, https://searchengineland.com/google-says-dont-include-fake-or-undisclosed-incentivized-reviews-in-review-snippet-structured-data-483456). Google published guidance stating that incentivized or undisclosed reviews should not be marked up with review snippet schema. This is a live-relevance item for the reviews-heavy corners of [[SEO Portfolio]], pest control and bus hire in particular, where review schema and star ratings are a meaningful part of the SERP presentation. Worth a quick internal check of whether any portfolio site markup relies on incentivized or gated reviews before Google starts actively suppressing snippets rather than just warning about them. Opened as a new thread below rather than a full story, since it broke Friday and there is no today-dated escalation yet.

**"AI search can't verify your business, here's how to fix it"** (Search Engine Land, 24 July 2026, https://searchengineland.com/ai-search-cant-verify-business-fix-483376). A piece on the gap between what AI answer engines can confirm about a local business (licensing, legitimacy, service area) versus what traditional local pack signals confirm. Directly relevant to [[Comparison Network]] and the local-intent niches in the portfolio, where trust signals matter for both classic local SEO and the newer AI-citation layer. Not urgent, but worth a proper read when the weekday cycle resumes and this can be verified as a trend rather than a one-off piece.
