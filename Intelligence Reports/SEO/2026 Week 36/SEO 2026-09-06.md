## Story 1: Cloudflare's 15 September crawler default reconfirmed — still the one live deadline, still unfixed

**Source:** Search Engine Journal, Cloudflare Blog, and related coverage (via search) | https://www.searchenginejournal.com/report-that-cloudflare-ai-bot-blocking-prevents-googlebot-from-indexing-sites/584673/, https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]], [[Comparison Network]]

Independent verification of the mechanic already carried in this report's open loops table: from 15 September, Cloudflare classifies Googlebot and Bingbot as dual-purpose "Search + Training" crawlers, and any domain that blocks AI-training crawlers on ad-carrying pages will block Googlebot and Bingbot on those same pages too, unless the site explicitly opts search back in. The rollout applies to new domains onboarding to Cloudflare, sites newly added by existing customers, and any Free-tier account that hasn't touched its settings — which covers a meaningful share of small-to-mid portfolio sites by default. This isn't new information, but it closes out any doubt about whether the previously reported mechanic was overstated: it wasn't.

For [[SEO Portfolio]] and [[Comparison Network]], this is still the single most concrete, dated action item on file: a five-minute per-domain Security tab check on Cloudflare, done before 15 September, against a real risk of losing organic indexing on ad-carrying pages by default. Nine days out and, per the standing note, still not confirmed done across the portfolio.

## Story 2: Google Ads and product mechanics sweep — AdSense AI Help guide, YouTube alcohol ads, Limited Ad Serving

**Source:** Search Engine Land, multiple articles dated 4 September | https://searchengineland.com/google-rolls-out-ai-powered-adsense-help-guide-to-all-english-help-center-traffic-487140, https://searchengineland.com/google-opens-youtube-personalized-ads-to-alcohol-advertisers-487133, https://searchengineland.com/limited-ad-serving-google-ads-486730
**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

Three Ads-side items landed Friday, grouped since none carries enough weight alone. Google rolled its AI-powered AdSense Help guide out to all English-language Help Center traffic, extending the pattern (already flagged in Thursday's report re: Google Photos) of Google making every first-party support and product surface agentic by default. YouTube opened personalised ads to alcohol advertisers, a targeting-category expansion with no immediate portfolio relevance. More useful for [[Comparison Network]]: Search Engine Land's explainer on "Limited Ad Serving" — an account-level Google Ads restriction (not a suspension or strike) that throttles impression opportunities, often triggered by targeting competitor brand names, running as an affiliate/reseller/lead-gen account, or accumulating negative-reputation signals. The case study cited: one account's impressions dropped from 800,000 to 350,000 monthly after being limited, with appeals taking "weeks, months, or even years."

Direct relevance: any comparison-network account running lead-gen or affiliate-style campaigns (a real description of parts of [[Comparison Network]]'s model) is a plausible candidate for this restriction if it targets competitor brand keywords or lacks strong own-brand presence on landing pages. Worth a one-off check against Google Ads account status for any comparison-network campaigns, not urgent but cheap to verify.
