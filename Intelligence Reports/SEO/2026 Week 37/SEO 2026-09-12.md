## Story 1: Google Search Console's June indexing data is gone for good, Mueller confirms

**Source:** Search Engine Land / Search Engine Roundtable, dated 11 September | https://searchengineland.com/google-search-console-indexing-report-missing-june-data-488261
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]]

Several days of June 2026 data are missing from the Page Indexing report in Search Console, with gaps scattered through the month's reporting graph. John Mueller confirmed the gap traces back to a period in June when indexing data itself was delayed at the time, and stated plainly that Google does not retroactively backfill missing indexing data; the team would look into it but the historical gap is permanent. He was clear this is a universal reporting artefact affecting all GSC users, not a sign of an actual indexing problem with any individual site.

Direct relevance for [[SEO Portfolio]]: if anyone pulled a June indexing trend line for any portfolio property and saw an unexplained dip, this is very likely the cause, not a real coverage loss. Worth noting in any historical indexing analysis so a permanent reporting hole doesn't get mistaken for a site-specific problem months from now.

## Story 2: Google Ads data confirms the shift to long-tail, conversational queries since AI Mode

**Source:** Search Engine Land, dated 11 September | https://searchengineland.com/google-ads-data-shows-query-length-shift-post-ai-mode-458162
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]], [[Comparison Network]]

New Google Ads data shows a genuine restructuring of search behaviour since AI Mode's rollout. Impression share for 1-2 word queries fell 18 points (42% to 24%), while 3-4 word queries rose 15 points (33% to 48%). The shift is sharper still in what actually converts: 1-2 word queries dropped from 62% to 52% of conversions, 3-4 word queries jumped from 20% to 46%, and 7+ word queries quadrupled from 1% to 4%. The analysis frames it as users increasingly typing natural-language, high-context queries, and notes conversion rates in the 3-4 word bucket have risen every quarter since.

This is the most actionable item in today's report. Any [[SEO Portfolio]] or [[Comparison Network]] property still built primarily around short head-term keyword targeting is optimising for a shrinking and lower-converting slice of demand; the data argues for prioritising long-tail, natural-language phrasing in both ad copy and on-page content, and for mirroring the actual language customers now use in queries rather than the compressed keyword phrasing SEO content has traditionally defaulted to.

## Story 3: Advertisers report Google Ads credits revoked after being spent

**Source:** Search Engine Land, dated 11 September | https://searchengineland.com/advertisers-report-google-ads-credits-being-revoked-after-spending-488330
**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]]

Multiple advertisers are reporting that Google Ads promotional credits are being clawed back after they have already been spent on live campaigns, rather than simply expiring unused. No official Google statement on scope or cause has been located.

Worth a five-minute check on any portfolio or [[Comparison Network]] Google Ads account currently running on a promotional credit balance, to confirm spend hasn't been retroactively reversed, before assuming a budget figure is accurate.
