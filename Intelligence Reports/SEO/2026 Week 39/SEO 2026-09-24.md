## Story 1: Google AI Overviews cross 60% of US queries for the first time

**Source:** Xponent21, corroborated by prior Comscore desktop tracking | https://xponent21.com/insights/google-ai-overviews-surpass-60-percent/
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]], [[Comparison Network]]

Fresh tracking puts Google AI Overviews at 60.32% of US queries as of 21 September — the first time the feature has cleared the 60% mark, roughly doubling its presence since August 2024. This sits alongside Comscore's separate Q2 2026 desktop measurement, which had AI Overviews at 39.4% in June, up from 25.8% a year earlier — different methodologies, same direction, and the newer 60%+ figure suggests the growth curve accelerated again through the summer rather than plateauing.

This is the number that should worry anyone running lead-gen or affordable-content sites more than any single algorithm update: an AI Overview answering the query directly removes the click before it happens, regardless of ranking position. For pet-transport, funeral-repatriation, close-protection-hire, global-bus-hire and pest-control-usa — all informational-intent-heavy niches — the practical response isn't "rank better," it's making sure content is structured to be the source an AI Overview cites rather than just a page it summarises around. Worth a technical audit pass across the portfolio checking schema markup, clear answer-first structure, and citation-friendly formatting, separate from and more urgent than the ongoing Cloudflare bot audit below.

## Story 2: Comscore data shows ChatGPT's AI-prompt dominance splintering to Gemini, Claude, YouTube and Reddit

**Source:** Digiday, The Desk (Comscore Q2 2026 AI Intelligence Report) | https://digiday.com/media/comscore-data-shows-how-ai-discovery-is-splintering-beyond-chatgpt/
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]], [[GitHub Library]]

Comscore's latest data (covering through June 2026, reported this week) shows ChatGPT's share of AI prompt volume falling from 70% in January to roughly 50% by June, with Gemini rising from 17% to 30% and Claude from 2% to 11%. On the citation side — which matters more directly for content strategy — YouTube now appears in 16% of AI answers versus Reddit's 10%, a reversal from earlier in the year when Reddit led.

Two implications worth separating. First, GEO/AEO strategy built around optimising for a single assistant (ChatGPT specifically) is already outdated — a fragmenting market means content needs to be citable across at least three distinct AI answer engines with different retrieval behaviours, not one. Second, YouTube's rising citation share is a genuine opening for the portfolio: video content (even simple explainer or FAQ-format video) is increasingly being pulled into AI answers ahead of Reddit-style forum content, which is a cheap lever none of the current SEO portfolio sites are pulling yet.

## Story 3: Cloudflare's AI crawler default split continues to carry real Googlebot risk — audit still outstanding

**Source:** Cloudflare blog, corroborated by multiple trade coverage | https://blog.cloudflare.com/content-independence-day-ai-options/
**Related vault notes:** [[SEO Portfolio]], [[Comparison Network]]

No new development today, but this is worth restating with sharper urgency than a bare open-loop line: since Cloudflare's 15 September default flip, new customers, new sites, and all existing free-tier accounts now sort incoming crawlers into Search, AI Training and AI Agent categories, with Training and Agent blocked by default on any page carrying ads. The catch, confirmed in this week's coverage, is that roughly 36% of all crawler traffic comes from mixed-use bots that blend Search and Training functions in one crawler — and Cloudflare applies the most restrictive rule to any multi-purpose bot. That means a site with Training blocking enabled can silently lose Googlebot on ad-monetised pages, with no separate warning that organic search crawling has been cut off.

This has been flagged for over a week without action. Given Story 1's AI Overview growth and Story 2's citation fragmentation both depend on the portfolio actually being crawled and indexed in the first place, this audit is now the single highest-leverage item sitting untouched across pet transport, funeral repatriation, close protection hire, global bus hire, pest control and the comparison network. It converges with two other dated deadlines below (Ahrefs' AI-adjusted volume cutover and the LSA billing change, both 30 September–1 October) into the same week.
