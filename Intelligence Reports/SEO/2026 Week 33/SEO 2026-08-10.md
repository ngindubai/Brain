This thread has run in every report since 1 August with the same shape: third-party volatility tools spiking, SEO community chatter about ranking shuffles, Google's own Status Dashboard staying clean. Today it's worth surfacing properly rather than another one-line carry-forward, because the picture has filled in enough to be useful. Reporting places the instability starting 1 August and continuing through at least 6 August, with publishers describing "abrupt traffic losses, unstable rankings and unusual Discover behaviour." Separately, broader 2026 traffic data (not new today, but relevant context) shows median publisher organic traffic down 10% year-on-year, concentrated in sites ranked roughly 100th to 10,000th rather than at the very top — the top 10 sites in a given niche actually grew traffic slightly. Two explanations are live and not mutually exclusive: an unannounced or unusually quiet core/spam update, or the structural effect of AI Overviews continuing to eat click-through — the same data shows organic CTR for top-ranking pages dropping 34-58% on queries that trigger an AI Overview. For mid-pack sites specifically, both effects point the same direction (fewer clicks reaching page one at all).

**Signal for Orwell:** Worth an actual check across the portfolio this week rather than continued passive tracking — pet-transport, funeral-repatriation, closeprotectionhure-com, global-bus-hire and pest-control-usa all sit in the "ranked but not top-10" band this pattern is hitting hardest.
