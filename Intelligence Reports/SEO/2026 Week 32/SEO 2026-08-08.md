## Story 2: ChatGPT Atlas shuts down tomorrow — last full day to export

**Source:** TechRepublic, 9to5Mac | https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-chatgpt-atlas-shutdown-migration/

**Related vault notes:** [[Agent Org]]

Not new information, but today is the operationally relevant checkpoint: OpenAI's Atlas browser (launched October 2025) shuts down for good tomorrow, 9 August. Bookmarks, browsing history, saved passwords, cookies and open tabs do not transfer automatically to anything else — OpenAI's own guidance is to export bookmarks as HTML and manually back up anything else before access ends. ChatGPT conversation history is unaffected since it lives in the account, not the browser. OpenAI is folding the browser-agent capability into ChatGPT and Codex directly rather than replacing Atlas with a like-for-like product.

This is a minor item for Orwell specifically — flagging only because if Atlas was used anywhere in the agent stack for browsing/research tasks, today is the deadline to pull anything stored locally in it before it goes dark tomorrow.

**Signal for Orwell:** If any workflow touches Atlas, export and back up today — access ends tomorrow with no automatic migration path.
