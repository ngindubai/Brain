## Story 1: Kimi K3's full weights land on Hugging Face, the largest open-weight release in history

**Source:** Kimi/Moonshot AI blog | https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k3 | additional detail via TechTimes and Interconnects | https://www.techi.com/kimi-k3-open-weights-inference-economics/ | https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k3-the-open-weights-escalation
**Related vault notes:** [[Agent Org]], [[Ventures]]

Moonshot AI published the full weights for Kimi K3 on Hugging Face at 00:00 UTC today, eleven days after the model's API launch at the World AI Conference in Shanghai. At 2.8 trillion total parameters (a sparse mixture-of-experts design, only 16 of 896 experts firing per token, roughly 50 billion active parameters), it's the largest open-weight model ever released, under a Modified MIT licence that permits commercial use, fine-tuning and self-hosting. The native MXFP4 safetensors release is a roughly 594GB download, but running it needs around 1.4 terabytes of fast memory even at four-bit precision, so most teams will reach it through inference providers rather than self-hosting until the community produces smaller quantised versions.

This lands in the same week as DeepSeek V4's stable release (24 July) and follows the open-weight policy coalition story tracked in recent reports, three separate signals in ten days that the open-weight frontier is moving fast and is not a US-labs-only conversation. For [[Ventures]]-style low-budget experimentation, the near-term practical impact is limited: K3 itself is too large to self-host without serious infrastructure, so the actual usable option remains K3 via a hosted inference provider or a smaller open-weight model once quantised community builds appear. Worth a note in [[Agent Org]] design thinking as a data point on how fast frontier-scale open weights are becoming available, not an action item this week.
