## Story 1: ChatGPT Enterprise workspaces on legacy weekly spend limits migrate to monthly today

**Source:** OpenAI Help Center | https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001001-manage-usage-limits-and-overages-in-chatgpt-enterprise-and-edu

**Related vault notes:** [[Products]]

From today, any ChatGPT Enterprise or Edu workspace still running OpenAI's legacy weekly role-based spend limits is automatically migrated to monthly limits. Existing monthly workspace, group or user settings take precedence; any weekly limit not already superseded gets its weekly figure multiplied by four to become the new monthly cap. Roles, permissions and feature access are unaffected. The migration is one-way — the legacy weekly configuration is removed afterward and cannot be reverted.

**Signal for Orwell:** No direct action item — nothing in the current stack runs on ChatGPT Enterprise seat-based spend controls. Filed as a data point on OpenAI continuing to simplify its enterprise billing model rather than anything requiring a decision.

## Story 2: IJCAI-ECAI 2026 opens in Bremen

**Source:** Web search, IJCAI 2026 conference site | https://2026.ijcai.org/

**Related vault notes:** [[Agent Org]]

It's one of the two or three largest academic AI venues of the year, alongside NeurIPS and ICML, and typically produces a wave of accepted-paper announcements and industry-lab keynote coverage over its run.
