## Story 1: Rainbow Six Tactics fully revealed, turn-based squad tactics enters the XCOM-adjacent field

**Source:** Strategy and Wargaming | https://strategyandwargaming.com/2026/08/29/rainbow-six-tactics-everything-we-know-so-far-features-operators-and-gameplay/
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

Ubisoft's Rainbow Six Tactics detail drop (29 Aug, first logged here) puts it squarely in the turn-based tactics lane that Star Wars Zero Company and the XCOM lineage occupy: breach planning, destructible environments and 15 launch operators drawn from the Rainbow Six roster. It is a genre bet rather than a franchise gimmick — Ubisoft is trying to convert Rainbow Six's operator-identity hook into a tactics-game structure rather than a shooter.

## Story 2: GTA 6 built a dedicated NPC animation team, 600,000+ animations, more than 10x GTA 5

**Source:** PC Gamer / GamesRadar / TechRadar (multi-source corroboration) | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/thanks-to-a-dedicated-team-art-directing-every-single-npc-gta-6-has-over-10-times-as-many-unique-animations-as-gta-5/
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

This detail surfaced 28-29 August via a New York Times interview and is still circulating, so it is logged here for the first time rather than as new news. Rockstar built a Los Angeles-based team of writers, directors and producers whose sole job was directing individual NPCs. Result: 600,000+ unique animations versus GTA 5's roughly 55,000 and Red Dead Redemption 2's roughly 300,000 — double RDR2's already industry-leading figure.

The relevance is the production model, not the animation count: a standing department dedicated to background-character fidelity is a budget and headcount commitment most open-world studios cannot match. It sets the bar GTA VI's Enhanced-mode successor to FiveM will eventually have to run alongside — NoPixel V and any RP framework rebuilding on GTA 6 tech inherits an NPC baseline nobody else can replicate.

**Source:** Cfx forum, Qbox GitHub, web search corroboration | https://forum.cfx.re/ ; https://github.com/Qbox-project/qbx_core/releases
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

Direct check of the framework NORTHLANDS runs on: Qbox core (`qbx_core`) is still at v1.24.0, dated 22 August, no new release for the ninth day running. One background item worth noting for the framework layer generally (not today-dated, happened early August per search corroboration): Cfx.re retired Keymaster in favour of the centralised Cfx.re Portal for license keys and asset management — the key-generation flow now asks for a display name only, no server IP binding, so any old advice about IP-locking keys is out of date. A separate FiveM-for-GTAV-Enhanced development update (originally posted 1 July, resurfaced in today's search pass, not new) confirms a coming .NET 10 runtime swap replacing Mono, a VoIP rebuild off Mumble, a WebSocket-based connection handshake, and a standalone Enhanced launcher — post-launch-scope changes that will eventually matter for any FiveM-based server's upgrade path, NORTHLANDS included, but nothing actionable yet.
