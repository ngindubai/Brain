## Story 1: Wardogs beta tops 200,000 concurrent Steam players, briefly the third-most-played game on the platform

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/as-wardogs-blows-past-200k-steam-concurrents-its-developers-are-prepared-for-the-day-hype-dies-down/
**Related vault notes:** [[Products]]

Wardogs — a 100-player, three-team tactical FPS from Bulkhead (published by Team17) combining infantry combat, vehicle warfare and destructible-battlefield outpost construction — crossed 200,000 concurrent players during its closed beta, briefly sitting third on Steam behind CS2 and Dota 2. Bulkhead CEO Joe Brammer's response was notably level-headed rather than triumphant: he called the spike "completely normal" for a beta and said the studio expects the number to fall away over the coming months, with the actual goal being a smaller, sustainable long-term community rather than sustaining a launch-week peak.

This sits just outside this report's core RTS/wargame/survival remit — it's a shooter, not a strategy game — but the outpost-construction layer gives it a base-building flavour worth tracking as a genre-crossover data point: another example of destructible-world, build-and-defend mechanics finding an audience well outside the pure survival-crafting space this report usually covers. No FiveM or NORTHLANDS relevance.
