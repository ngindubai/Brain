## Story 1: Wardogs playtest sets a new peak at 245,118 concurrent players, still climbing four days before Early Access

**Source:** SteamDB via X | https://x.com/SteamDB/status/2095993575699837018
**Related vault notes:** [[Products]]

Wardogs — Team17-published Bulkhead's 100-player tactical FPS with destructible-battlefield outpost construction — logged a fresh concurrent-player peak of 245,118 on 5 September, up from the 200K mark flagged in yesterday's report and well above the second closed beta's 162,488 peak. The climb hasn't plateaued yet, which is the notable part: CEO Joe Brammer's public expectation was for the number to fall away post-beta, and instead it's still rising four days before the 10 September Early Access launch. Still outside this report's core RTS/wargame remit, but the outpost-construction layer keeps it worth tracking as a base-building-adjacent data point — a shooter pulling numbers usually reserved for CS2/Dota 2-tier live-service titles.

No FiveM or NORTHLANDS relevance.

## Story 2: NoPixel V closed beta two days out — final details confirmed

**Source:** Massively Overpowered | https://massivelyop.com/2026/09/03/grand-theft-auto-onlines-official-roleplaying-server-nopixel-v-enters-closed-beta-september-8/
**Related vault notes:** none directly

No new development since yesterday, but the countdown is now close enough to note the confirmed shape of it: NoPixel V — the Rockstar-backed successor built on the FiveM framework Rockstar acquired in 2023 — opens closed beta on the Rockstar Games Launcher on 8 September, invite-only to roughly 450 content creators and established roleplayers in the first phase. The promotional campaign (up to GTA$1,500,000 and a Burger Shot Tracksuit via Twitch Drops) runs 8-30 September. This is the biggest FiveM-adjacent event on the calendar this month; expect a full write-up once the beta actually opens Tuesday.

## Story 3: BlizzCon opens in six days — Blizzard's teasing points to a StarCraft-universe shooter, not StarCraft 3

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/its-really-starting-to-look-like-a-new-starcraft-game-will-be-revealed-at-blizzcon/
**Related vault notes:** none directly

No new teaser today, but the picture sharpened enough this week to be worth a clean note before the 12 September opening ceremony: the incoming reveal is reported to be a third-person shooter set roughly 70 years after StarCraft II (in-universe year 2575), built in Unreal Engine 5, explicitly not StarCraft 3, not a mobile game, and not from Nexon. The team folds in veterans from the cancelled Project Odyssey alongside outside hires including Dan Hay (Far Cry) and Drew Murray (Sunset Overdrive, Spider-Man 2). Outside this report's RTS focus by genre, but the StarCraft brand carries enough weight in the strategy space to flag ahead of the reveal.

## Story 4: Two strategy-adjacent Early Access launches land this week

**Source:** Steam Community | https://steamcommunity.com/games/2445260/announcements/detail/696522455474241788
**Related vault notes:** none directly

Stoke Games' free-to-play multiplayer tank shooter Tyr entered Early Access on 2 September after more than three years of playtesting — positioning-heavy tank combat rather than traditional RTS, but close enough to this report's sweep to flag. A colony-sim dungeon crawler also launched into Early Access on 4 September, tasking players with building a settlement and managing expeditions into a hostile dungeon below it — a genuine base-building/survival hybrid, smaller-scale than the genre's bigger 2026 releases but worth a name-check for anyone tracking the crowded colony-sim space this year.
