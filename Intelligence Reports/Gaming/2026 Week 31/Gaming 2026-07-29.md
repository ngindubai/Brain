## 1. Company of Heroes 3: Final Stand launches on Steam

**Source:** Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/4836540/Company_of_Heroes_3_Final_Stand/ ; DLH.NET | https://www.dlh.net/en/news/89619/company-of-heroes-3-final-stand-launches-29-july.html

Relic's standalone roguelite wave-defense spin-off of Company of Heroes 3 released today at $29.99 (10% launch discount, extra 20% bundle discount for existing CoH3 owners). No prior CoH game is required. It features 12 escalating rounds, 36 boss units, 18 dynamic events, an Endless Mode, and four playable factions (US Forces, Wehrmacht, British Forces, Deutsches Afrikakorps), playable solo or two-player co-op. Pre-launch community sentiment on Steam was mixed, with some players wanting new campaign content rather than a wave-defense spin-off, and pre-launch PC Gamer coverage described it as feeling more like a mode than a $29.99 standalone product. Launch-day review and Steam user-review data hadn't caught up at time of research — worth a same-day or next-day follow-up once player reception lands.

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/gta-online-in-game-gambling-is-now-blocked-in-australia/
