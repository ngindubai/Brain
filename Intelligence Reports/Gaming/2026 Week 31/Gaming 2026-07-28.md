## 1. Base-building double launch: "Sir, We Have an Orc Problem" and "Skyward Bastion" both hit Steam today

**Source:** Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/4594150/Sir_We_Have_an_Orc_Problem/ ; Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/3682650

Two tower-defense/base-building titles released today. Frantic's "Sir, We Have an Orc Problem" is an incremental-style base-defence game where players place and upgrade turrets, call in artillery and orbital-laser strikes, and hold out against waves of up to 120,000 orcs in a physics-driven combat sim. Separately, "Skyward Bastion" is a tower-defense roguelite where players build and expand a floating-island base against endless waves across procedurally-expanding maps with a day-night cycle. Both are day-one releases with no review data yet. Neither is NORTHLANDS-relevant — both single-player PvE.

## 2. "Island Beekeeper" launches as a cosy open-world survival-craft title

**Source:** Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/3968160/Island_Beekeeper/

Akuseru Studio's open-world survival-craft game released today after an earlier delay for technical issues. Players build an apiary on floating islands, breed bees, and construct a home in the sky; priced $11.99 with a launch-week discount to $8.99. Fits the survival/base-building brief at the cosy end rather than hardcore survival. Not NORTHLANDS-relevant.
