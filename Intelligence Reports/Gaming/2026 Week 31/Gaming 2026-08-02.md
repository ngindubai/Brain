## 1. EA's $55bn go-private deal set to close Tuesday 4 August
**Source:** GamingBolt | https://gamingbolt.com/eas-acquisition-by-saudi-arabias-pif-gets-regulatory-approval-deal-will-close-on-august-4th ; SEC filing coverage via Shacknews | https://www.shacknews.com/article/150204/ea-merger-regulatory-approval-closing-date

EA confirmed in a 30 July SEC filing that every regulatory approval needed for its $55bn take-private sale is in hand, with the European Commission clearing it on antitrust grounds on 23 July. The deal is expected to close "on or about the close of trading" this Tuesday, ending EA's 35-year run as a public company. Ownership splits PIF 93.4%, Silver Lake 5.5%, Affinity Partners 1.1%; Andrew Wilson stays as CEO and EA's Redwood City HQ is unchanged. This is the largest leveraged buyout on record for the industry.

No NORTHLANDS relevance directly, but worth watching Tuesday's actual close for any statement on studio strategy — a PIF-controlled EA going private removes public-market pressure on live-service monetisation, which could cut either way for competing survival/RP titles.
