## 1. EA's $55bn Saudi PIF/Silver Lake go-private deal set to close 4 August
**Source:** Kotaku | https://kotaku.com/ea-says-sale-saudi-arabia-go-through-next-week-2000720596 ; GameSpot | https://www.gamespot.com/articles/ea-says-saudi-takeover-deal-is-set-to-close-next-week/

Electronic Arts confirmed today that all regulatory approvals needed for its acquisition by a consortium of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, Silver Lake Partners and Affinity Partners (owned by Jared Kushner) have been obtained, with the deal set to close on or about 4 August. At $55bn, it is the largest leveraged buyout in history, loading EA with roughly $20bn in debt from JPMorgan Chase financing. Andrew Wilson stays on as CEO. Worth watching for the industry-wide precedent: sovereign-wealth-backed take-privates of major publishers are no longer hypothetical, and EA is the biggest test case yet of what changes (or doesn't) under that ownership structure.

## 2. Xbox revenue falls $1.7bn (7%) for FY26; hardware down 29%, fourth straight declining quarter
**Source:** Kotaku | https://kotaku.com/xbox-revenue-dropped-1-7-billion-last-year-2000720316 ; Game World Observer | https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/07/30/xboxs-annual-revenue-has-declined-but-satya-nadella-remains-optimistic

Microsoft's FY26 results, reported alongside yesterday's Azure headline numbers, showed Xbox revenue down $1.7bn (7%) for the year, with content and services down 5% and hardware down 29%. Q4 alone was worse: $4.98bn in gaming revenue, down 10% YoY, the lowest quarterly total since fiscal Q1 2024. This follows July's 4,800 job cuts (3,200 of them in Xbox). Satya Nadella and gaming chief Asha Sharma both maintain growth returns in FY27, but Sharma's own public comments point to a slow recovery rather than a quick rebound. Not NORTHLANDS-relevant directly, but a useful data point on where Microsoft's platform priorities are likely to sit relative to its AI/cloud spend covered in today's [[Markets]] report.

## 3. Valve launches Deadlock's first proper ranked mode
**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/moba/valve-finally-gives-deadlock-a-proper-ranked-mode-warns-that-matches-will-be-very-rocky-to-start/

Valve rolled out a genuine matchmaking overhaul for Deadlock on 30 July, splitting the game into separate Standard and Ranked queues for the first time. Ranked requires 60 Standard wins to unlock, and each hero must individually have 15 Standard wins before it's usable in Ranked — a deliberate mastery gate. Season one runs 30 July to 7 October. Valve itself has warned early matches will be "very rocky." No NORTHLANDS relevance; noted as a genuine feature-complete update rather than routine content, unlike most of what passes for "news" in the live-service space day to day.
