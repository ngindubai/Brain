## 1. Company of Heroes 3: Final Stand — first reviews land
**Source:** PCGamesN | https://www.pcgamesn.com/company-of-heroes-3-final-stand/review-wave-defense ; AltChar | https://www.altchar.com/reviews/company-of-heroes-3-final-stand-review-a-solid-twist-on-a-ww2-classic-if-a-little-thin-on-content-aBWkE0a8WGFf

First reviews of yesterday's standalone roguelite wave-defense spin-off went up today. PCGamesN scores it 8/10, calling it "an easy recommendation" for series veterans and newcomers alike, praising the tight ~40-minute matches and the way the base game's cover/suppression systems turn survival into a tactical puzzle. AltChar is cooler — "a solid twist... if a little thin on content" — flagging only five maps total and repetitiveness for players who stick to one faction.

Consensus across both: a well-executed experiment rather than a system-seller, limited by content depth rather than design. No relevance to NORTHLANDS; noted only as the promised follow-up to yesterday's launch story.
