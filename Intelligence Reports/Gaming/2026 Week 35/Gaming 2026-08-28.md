## Stories

### 1. GTA 6 Extended Look airs: map twice GTA 5's size, new criminal profile and disguise systems, Netflix reportedly crashes on demand

**Source:** PCGamer / Variety | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/the-gta-6-netflix-exclusive-reveal-is-on-youtube-now/ | https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/gta-6-netflix-trailer-extended-look-release-1236845475/

The 26-27 minute Netflix exclusive aired yesterday at 3pm ET and is now on YouTube for anyone without a subscription. Demand reportedly crashed Netflix at the moment it went live, and the stream drew 1.8 million concurrent viewers on Twitch — a real data point on how much appetite remains for this game nearly ten months after the leak wave broke. Substance-wise: Vice City's map is now confirmed at roughly twice Los Santos' size from GTA 5 and three times Red Dead Redemption 2's map, Rockstar built new tools for character variety and population density, and the gameplay showed a criminal profile/wanted-level redesign, tactical shooting, character hot-swapping between Jason and Lucia, and Vice City side activities including scuba diving and basketball. Reception is broadly positive on the setting and mechanics, with the recurring caveat (echoed across PCGamer and GamesBeat) that story missions still look structurally similar to prior Rockstar titles — "talk then shoot." Release date holds at 19 November 2026, unchanged.
