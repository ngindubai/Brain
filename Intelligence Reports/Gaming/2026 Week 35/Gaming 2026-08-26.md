## Stories

### 1. Gamescom Opening Night Live breaks the RTS drought: Heroes of Might and Magic III remake is the surprise of the night

**Source:** TechRadar / GameInformer / play.asia (via search) | https://www.techradar.com/gaming/everything-announced-atgamescom-opening-night-live-2026

Ubisoft used last night's Opening Night Live to reveal a full remake of Heroes of Might and Magic III, built internally at Ubisoft Paris — widely called the surprise of the show. The 1999 turn-based strategy classic is being rebuilt in full 3D with all nine factions and their units redesigned, a rotatable adventure-map camera, a reworked interface giving war machines and artefacts dedicated sections, and the original campaigns woven into one unified story with a new prologue, epilogue and cinematics. Online battles and eight-player hot-seat mode are both confirmed. It's coming to PS5, Xbox Series X/S and PC (Steam and Ubisoft Store); no release date yet. This is the single biggest strategy-genre announcement this brief has logged since it started tracking the release drought, and it's a full turn-based 4X/strategy title rather than an RTS proper — still squarely inside this brief's remit.

### 2. Game of Thrones: War for Westeros gets its first gameplay trailer — RTS proper, four playable factions

**Source:** GameInformer / Gamingtrend / play.asia (via search) | https://gameinformer.com/gamescom-2026/2026/08/25/heres-the-first-gameplay-trailer-for-game-of-thrones-war-for-westeros

PlaySide Studios' Game of Thrones: War for Westeros debuted its first gameplay footage at Opening Night Live (8pm CEST broadcast slot), showing real-time skirmish battles across four playable factions — House Stark, House Lannister, House Targaryen and the White Walkers — commanded alongside HBO-likeness heroes including Jon Snow, Daenerys Targaryen, Jaime Lannister and the Night King. The game has slipped from its original 2026 window to early 2027 on PC/Steam, with a beta confirmed to open sign-ups before year-end. This is the RTS proper this brief has been waiting on — a genuine real-time strategy title with a licensed setting and a firm platform commitment, not a remaster or a leak.

### 3. Paradox accidentally leaks its own next grand-strategy game, Afterworld, hours before the official reveal

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/strategy/paradox-commits-a-classic-blunder-and-accidentally-leaks-its-own-post-apocalyptic-strategy-game/ | TheSixthAxis (via search)

Paradox Development Studio's next original grand-strategy title, Afterworld, leaked via the company's own forum ahead of its planned Opening Night Live debut — undercutting the reveal it had been teasing. Set in a post-apocalyptic North America, Afterworld mixes Paradox's usual grand-strategy empire-building with Fallout/Mad Max-style survivor-tribe management: hazardous terrain, old ruins, moral crises alongside the strategic layer. It's led by Dan Lind, former lead of Hearts of Iron IV, and is only the second wholly fictional setting from Paradox Development Studio after Stellaris. Three strategy-genre reveals in one 24-hour window — after weeks of this brief logging a drought — is the clearest signal yet that the genre's next wave is queuing up behind Gamescom rather than being actually dead.

The "Cyberleek" leak wave — running daily since 18 August — hit its one-week mark yesterday, with total leaked footage now around 17 minutes across a playable build, spanning a tour of Vice City including gas stations, strip clubs, nudist villages, beaches and clubs.
