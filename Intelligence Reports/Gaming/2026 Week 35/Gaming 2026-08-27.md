## Stories

### 1. GTA VI: An Extended Look premieres on Netflix today, Rockstar's first real reply to the leaks

**Source:** Forbes / Vice / Netflix Tudum | https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/08/26/netflixs-gta-6-extended-look-isnt-the-only-gta-preview-tomorrow | https://www.netflix.com/tudum/articles/grand-theft-auto-6-extended-first-look

Rockstar's "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" streams exclusively on Netflix today at 12pm PT/3pm ET, captured entirely from in-game PS5 footage and expected to run 20-30 minutes — the deepest official look at the game yet. It stays Netflix-exclusive for six hours before going free on Rockstar's own social channels, then hits the official Rockstar YouTube channel at 9pm ET. The timing matters: this is Rockstar's first substantial on-its-own-terms GTA VI content since the "Cyberleek" leak wave began on 18 August, and it's a deliberate reassertion of control over the narrative rather than a scheduled marketing beat that happened to land during a leak crisis. GTA VI's confirmed release date remains 19 November 2026, unchanged and unrelated to either the leaks or today's stream.

### 2. GTA 6 leak saga: Take-Two's subpoena deadline holds at 4 September, no ID on the leaker

**Source:** Tom's Hardware / Kotaku | https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/console-gaming/take-two-subpoenas-microsoft-for-windows-device-ids-of-everyone-in-three-discord-servers-in-gta-6-leak-hunt

No movement today on identifying "Cyberleek." Take-Two's DMCA subpoenas, filed 20 August in the Southern District of New York against Microsoft and Discord, seek device IDs, login IPs, phone numbers and linked account data for everyone who posted in three named Discord servers, with a 4 September compliance deadline. Discord has stated it hasn't yet been formally served. This continues to run in parallel with, not resolved by, today's official Netflix reveal — expect Rockstar to keep leaning on today's controlled content rather than the legal track to manage the narrative in the near term.

**Source:** PureXbox / GameSpot (via search) | https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/08/xboxs-first-gamescom-2026-stream-included-two-massive-reveals-and-multiple-new-games

Xbox's three-hour Gamescom stream today delivered 18 minutes of new Fable gameplay and a fresh look at Gears of War: E-Day, alongside dates and pricing for the Xbox Series X25 console and controller and a Disc-to-Digital program supporting thousands of physical Xbox titles. None of this touches the RTS/grand-strategy or FiveM beats this brief tracks closely, but it's the volume story of the day industry-wide — Gamescom's show floor opened today too, at a record 233,000 square metres across roughly 1,600 exhibitors from 67 countries, sold out for the first time in the event's history. The Gamescom Awesome Indies Show runs tonight at 8pm CEST, the next scheduled slot worth watching for any late strategy-genre surprise.
