## Stories

### 1. Take-Two subpoenas Microsoft and Discord over the GTA 6 leaks
**Source:** Kotaku, Variety, Yahoo News (via web search, PCGamer/IGN/RPS coverage of this was unreachable directly) | https://kotaku.com/take-two-subpoenas-microsoft-and-discord-records-related-to-spread-of-gta-6-leaks-2000726633

Take-Two filed subpoenas in the US District Court for the Southern District of New York on 20 August, using DMCA provisions to compel Microsoft and Discord to hand over identifying data tied to the "Cyberleek" persona: account IDs, registration/login IPs, phone numbers, device identifiers, and Discord server membership data going back to 1 June. Response deadline is 4 September. The infringing content was first posted via GitHub (a Microsoft platform), which is the hook for the Microsoft subpoena. Microsoft's Xbox CTO says it's "working closely with Take-Two and Rockstar," Discord says it complies with valid DMCA subpoenas as standard. This is the first time the leak saga has moved from takedown notices into a formal identification process with a real court deadline, a genuine escalation from where this stood at the weekend.
