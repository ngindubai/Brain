### 3. Leaked DLSS 5 keeps spreading through modded games, with mixed and sometimes unsettling results

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/graphics-cards/claude-strife-dlss-5-has-escaped-into-the-wild-and-things-are-immediately-weird/
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]], [[Agent Org]]

Data miners pulled a DLSS 5 neural-rendering DLL out of NBA 2K27's early access build on 26 August, and modders have since patched it to run on older RTX 40-series cards and dropped it into unrelated titles. PC Gamer's piece today documents the results on stylised character models: the generative filter — Nvidia's confirmed "ship this fall" tech — imposes a photorealistic "AI-generated look" on faces built for a cartoon or painterly art style, producing uncanny results players are calling "horrifying" elsewhere in coverage. Early performance testing shows a real cost too: an RTX 5070 Ti dropped from 71 to 35 FPS at 4K running the leaked build in Control. Not FiveM/NORTHLANDS-relevant, but worth tracking as a signal on how neural-rendering upscaling actually behaves once players get their hands on it ahead of official launch, relevant to any Orwell-adjacent product thinking about generative visual tooling.
