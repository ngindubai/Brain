## Stories

### 1. Gamescom Opening Night Live airs today, 2pm ET — line-up confirmed, results not yet in
**Source:** Various via web search (Yahoo Tech, ScreenRant, Red Bull, AltChar) | https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/watch-gamescom-opening-night-live-142608631.html

Geoff Keighley and Eefje Depoortere host tonight's two-hour Opening Night Live stream at 11am PT/2pm ET, kicking off Gamescom's public show week (floor opens 26-30 August after a confirmed sellout). Confirmed segments include another Final Fantasy VII Revelation look with director Naoki Hamaguchi, a world-premiere story trailer for Gears of War: E-Day, The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt's "Songs of the Past" expansion, Metro 2039, Path of Exile 2, and a Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons reveal, with Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 widely expected to feature. None of the confirmed line-up so far is RTS or grand strategy — this is a tactics/RPG/shooter-heavy show — so the genre drought this brief has tracked all month is unlikely to break tonight. Because this research runs ahead of the 2pm ET broadcast, tomorrow's report carries the actual reveals and any surprise strategy-genre announcements.

### 2. WARDOGS closed beta wraps with a genuine number: nearly 500,000 players
**Source:** PCGamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/rss/
**Related:** yesterday's report flagged the beta window closing with no post-mortem numbers yet

The milsim extraction shooter's closed beta (window closed 24 August 02:00 UTC, as reported yesterday) drew close to 500,000 players, a genuinely large turnout for a beta in this niche. That's a strong signal for a 10 September early access launch at $39.99 — not RTS/grand strategy proper, but worth logging given the scale, and a useful comparison point for anyone tracking milsim/extraction-shooter community sizing against NORTHLANDS' own FiveM population.

### 3. GTA 6 leak saga: sixth wave gets more explicit, legal escalation still pending
**Source:** PCGamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/rss/

Today's PCGamer coverage of the ongoing "Cyberleek" leak wave notes newly surfaced footage showing an uncensored, fully nude location model — a step up in explicitness from the vehicle and interior footage in prior batches. This is an incremental update to the story tracked extensively in yesterday's report, not a new saga: Take-Two's subpoenas against Microsoft and Discord (filed 20 August, response deadline 4 September) are unchanged today, and there's no confirmation yet of the leaker's identity. Netflix's "Extended Look" is still scheduled for 27 August; each new leak wave narrows the odds it airs to a genuinely uninformed audience.
