## Story 3: GTA 5 "Flock cameras" mod surfaces in today's feed pull, dated 11 September

**Source:** PC Gamer (via RSS today) | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/modder-adds-235-destructible-flock-cameras-to-gta-5-so-you-can-do-whatever-the-flock-you-want-to-them/

Flagging this transparently: cross-checking shows the original coverage is dated 11 September, not today, so it does not get full breaking treatment, it just happened to appear in today's PC Gamer RSS pull. Modder "Morry" (WTTDOTM) added 235 functioning Flock-style surveillance cameras across Los Santos via a mod called GTALPR, they photograph passing vehicles, can alert police even without a violation (mirroring real-world Flock camera complaints), and are destructible for cash plus built-in speedrun timing. It's a single-player ScriptHook/OpenIV mod, not a FiveM server resource, so there is no direct NORTHLANDS action item. Noted mainly because it shows the GTA 5 modding scene is still actively shipping ambitious new tooling seven-plus years post-launch and two months out from GTA VI, which is the more relevant signal for the FiveM ecosystem's long tail.
