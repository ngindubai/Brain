## Story 1: Wardogs launches into Steam Early Access today

**Source:** GameWatcher | https://www.gamewatcher.com/wardogs-release-date and Insider Gaming | https://insider-gaming.com/wardogs-come-out-early-access-1-0-launch/

BULKHEAD's Wardogs — 100-player large-scale tactical warfare with destructible environments and vehicles, published by Team17 — went live in Steam Early Access today at 16:00 UTC, priced $39.99, with a Supporter Edition also available. The closed beta that preceded it drew 245,000 players before wrapping up last week. BULKHEAD has said it expects to stay in Early Access for 12-24 months depending on feedback — a more conservative public timeline than most large multiplayer shooters commit to. This is the actual launch this report has been counting down since Tuesday, not a further day-out update.
