## Story 1: BlizzCon 2026 reveals new StarCraft as an open-world shooter, not the RTS fans expected

**Source:** GamesRadar | https://www.gamesradar.com/news/live/blizzcon-2026-everything-announced/ | Dexerto | https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/blizzard-reveals-new-starcraft-at-blizzcon-2026-3408565/ | Shacknews | https://www.shacknews.com/article/150708/starcraft-announcement-blizzcon-2026

Blizzard confirmed a new StarCraft title at Saturday's Opening Ceremony, its first new mainline entry since StarCraft 2 wrapped development in 2020. The dominant reporting, from GamesRadar and Dexerto, describes it as an open-world shooter for 2030, no gameplay shown yet, matching the pre-ceremony leak flagged in yesterday's report (an alleged "StarCraft Infantry" project helmed by ex-Far Cry executive producer Dan Hay). Shacknews, by contrast, described it as an RTS reboot "simply titled StarCraft." Given the majority of outlets and the pre-show ARG evidence (Terran marines, Mengsk's Dominion, a timeline before SC2) both point the same direction, treat the shooter framing as the more likely accurate read and the Shacknews line as probable early miscategorisation, but this is genuinely unresolved between sources and worth a same-day correction if Blizzard issues an official press kit with clearer genre language. Either way, this is a notable case study in a legacy RTS IP being repositioned as an action title rather than a strategy sequel, relevant background for anyone thinking about how established franchises get stretched into new genres.

## Story 2: Diablo 5 and a new WoW "Classic Plus" expansion also announced at BlizzCon

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/rss/

Diablo 5 was confirmed for a spring 2029 release, set a century after Diablo 4 with Sanctuary having fallen to Diablo; Blizzard says no further Diablo 4 expansions are planned, though seasonal content continues in the meantime. Separately, World of Warcraft's long-rumoured "Classic Plus" branch, titled WoW Forever, launches 4 November with three new zones, nine dungeons and two raids built around untold vanilla-era stories; beta access starts this week. Warcraft 3: Forsaken Kingdom, the franchise's first new campaign in 23 years, is also out now for Reforged owners at $30 alongside a free 3.0 engine update. None of this touches NORTHLANDS directly, but it's the clearest signal yet that Blizzard is leaning hard into legacy-IP nostalgia content (Warcraft 3, Classic Plus) at the same time as it repositions StarCraft into new territory, two different playbooks running in parallel at the same company.

## Story 3: Total War: Warhammer 40,000 controller support sparks RTS-on-console debate

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/rss/

Creative Assembly developer Dave Petry said today that RTS "really does work" with a controller, pointing to the studio's Halo Wars 2 pedigree as evidence the genre isn't inherently unsuited to gamepads, ahead of Total War: Warhammer 40,000's console-inclusive design. It's a small, single-source item, not a release or patch, but it's the one genuinely today-dated piece in the RTS/wargame lane and worth a line for anyone tracking whether the grand-strategy genre is being deliberately built for console audiences going forward, a trend line relevant to the genre's addressable market generally.
