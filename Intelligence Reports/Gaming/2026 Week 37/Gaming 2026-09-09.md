## Story 1: Valheim exits Early Access after five years — "Deep North" 1.0 live today

**Source:** TechTimes | https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326887/20260907/valheim-10-launches-september-9-free-update-deep-north-17-million-owners.htm and The FPS Review | https://www.thefpsreview.com/2026/06/09/valheim-1-0-has-a-release-date-september-9-2026-and-the-deep-north-is-finally-here/

Valheim's 1.0 update went live today at 6am PDT, closing out just over five years in Early Access for a game that has sold to roughly 17 million owners. The centrepiece is the Deep North biome — a frozen region at the world's northern edge that developer Iron Gate says carries "two biomes worth" of new content: a Gammeltrolls enemy type, Elakingar underground areas, new weapons and building pieces. The release is free for existing owners, save data carries over untouched, and the update adds full crossplay across PC, Xbox, PlayStation 5 and the Nintendo Switch 2 — the platform expansion is as significant as the content itself, since it's Valheim's first appearance on PlayStation and Switch 2 at all.

Squarely in this report's primary survival/base-building sweep, and a clean release rather than a contested one — no early bug reports or server-load complaints had surfaced as of writing. Worth a same-day mention rather than tomorrow's report given today is the actual launch date this report has been counting down to for weeks.

## Story 3: Wardogs' Early Access launch one day out

**Source:** GameWatcher | https://www.gamewatcher.com/wardogs-release-date and Insider Gaming | https://insider-gaming.com/wardogs-come-out-early-access-1-0-launch/

BULKHEAD's Wardogs — 100-player large-scale tactical warfare with destructible environments and vehicles, published by Team17 — enters Steam Early Access tomorrow, 10 September, at 16:00 UTC, priced at $39.99. The developers have said they expect to stay in Early Access for 12-24 months depending on player feedback, which is a more conservative EA timeline than most large multiplayer shooters commit to publicly. Genuinely one day out now rather than the two-day countdown this report carried yesterday — no change in substance, just the calendar moving.

## Story 5: CyberLeek GTA VI leak investigation edges toward a named suspect

**Source:** Kotaku | https://kotaku.com/the-hunt-for-the-gta-6-leaker-seems-to-be-getting-closer-to-its-target-2000728088 and TechRadar | https://www.techradar.com/gaming/teenager-involved-with-hack-which-led-to-gta-6-leak-court-finds

The hunt for the anonymous leaker behind August's CyberLeek GTA VI gameplay and map leaks has narrowed: investigators and Take-Two's lawyers are reportedly homing in on a Discord user, "stayonthegrindd," as either CyberLeek themselves or connected to the group. Separately, a UK court has found a teenager was involved in the underlying hack, though this reads as tied to the broader Lapsus$-adjacent hacking scene rather than a confirmed CyberLeek identity. No arrest has been confirmed. CyberLeek's three demands — no digital pre-orders, no fake single-player DLC padding, mandatory offline single-player fallback — remain unmet and unanswered by Rockstar.

Worth tracking as the subpoena and any arrest are the next real checkpoints; GTA VI itself remains on track for 19 November, a little over two months out.
