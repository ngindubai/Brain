## Story 1: NoPixel V closed beta goes live on the Rockstar Games Launcher

**Source:** TweakTown | https://www.tweaktown.com/news/113390/new-rockstar-backed-gta-v-rp-server-nopixel-v-premieres-sept-8-with-closed-beta-streams/index.html and Out of Games | https://outof.games/news/9751-rockstar-officially-backs-nopixel-v-with-closed-beta-launching-september-8/
**Related vault notes:** [[Products]]

NoPixel V's closed beta opened today, invite-only to 439 creators and established roleplayers, running on the Rockstar Games Launcher on PC. Named streamers confirmed with access include Valkyrae, xQc and Pokimane; access for everyone else is watch-only via their streams, not hands-on. The build carries a reworked character creator plus new and revamped locations — Pixel Hotel & Casino is new, Cypress Flats has been overhauled — after close to three years of development in direct collaboration with Rockstar. A Twitch Drops campaign runs from today through 30 September: watching eligible streams earns up to GTA$1,500,000 and an exclusive Burger Shot Tracksuit cosmetic, usable back in regular GTA Online.

This is the first FiveM-framework product Rockstar has put its own name on since acquiring Cfx.re in 2023, and a closed, streamer-gated beta is the right way to stress-test a server at this profile before any wider release. No launch-day technical reporting (queues, crashes, server stability) had surfaced as of writing — worth a follow-up check tomorrow once streamers have had a full session on it. For NORTHLANDS specifically, nothing here is an immediate action item since this is Rockstar's own server rather than a framework or Qbox release, but the character-creator and location design choices are worth watching as a signal of what Rockstar considers "good" FiveM roleplay design now that it owns the underlying tech.

**Related vault notes:** none directly

No change from yesterday's tracker, but worth restating with the countdown now at one day: Valheim's 1.0 release, subtitled "Deep North," lands tomorrow, 9 September, closing out more than five years in Early Access.
