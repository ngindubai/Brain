## Stories

### 1. Call of Duty: NEXT airs, Modern Warfare 4 Early Access Beta goes live

**Source:** Call of Duty official blog | https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/08/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-next-early-access-beta-weekend-one

Confirmed as scheduled: CoD NEXT streamed at 9am PT, and the Modern Warfare 4 Early Access Beta launched two hours later at 11am PT, both today. The reveal covered five 6v6 maps (Rooftops, Silkworm, Transit 213, Cachette, Lotus), six core modes including a new "Inflation" mode, and — a first for a CoD beta — a playable campaign mission alongside multiplayer. New systems shown: a "Mobility Course" training area, "Apex Attachments," and a "Kill Block" mechanic. Warzone Resurgence with a new Zodiac map is confirmed for Weekend Two. Activision is billing it as the "biggest CoD Beta ever." Early access runs to 25 August for pre-order holders; open beta follows 28 August–1 September for everyone including Switch 2; full release is 23 October.

No NORTHLANDS/FiveM relevance — different engine, different publisher — but it's the largest single volume of live-service competition landing this quarter, and it sets the marketing pace Rockstar will be answering with its own GTA 6 Netflix reveal on 27 August.

### 2. WARDOGS closed beta opens — large-scale tactical FPS with destruction and base-building

**Source:** worthplaying.com, neonsect.com/wardogs, pcgamesn.com (via search; not directly fetchable)

Closed beta for WARDOGS opened today and runs to 23 August, ahead of a September Steam Early Access launch. It's an "All-Out Warfare" shooter — up to 100 players, King-of-the-Hill-style control zones across 2x2km maps, with destruction and building mechanics layered onto combined-arms tactical combat. Flagging this against the primary RTS/wargame/base-building brief with an honest caveat: it's a shooter, not RTS or grand strategy — genuinely adjacent to the brief's focus, but it doesn't break the drought below.
