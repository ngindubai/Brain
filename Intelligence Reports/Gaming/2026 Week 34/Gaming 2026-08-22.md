## Stories

### 1. FiveM's FXServer 35245 baseline starts a hard countdown to 15 October
**Source:** Cfx Forum (Announcements) | https://forum.cfx.re/t/support-for-gta-online-the-kortz-center-heist-update-is-available-now/5422124

Posted 20 August and not caught in the prior sweep (which read the FiveM/QBox situation as clean off an April note), this is worth flagging properly now. FXServer build 35245 has become the new recommended baseline on the canary channel, adding support for GTA Online's Kortz Center Heist title update. The change is not cosmetic: the client now runs the latest stable GTA V executable by default and only loads Title Update content a server explicitly requests via `sv_enforceGameBuild`, rather than always shipping everything. Cfx has set 15 October 2026 as the point at which the old connection behaviour stops working entirely.

That gives any server still pinned to an older build roughly eight weeks to move across. Replies on the thread are less enthusiastic than the announcement: persistent symlink file issues, network thread hitching, and a long-standing YMT (asset streaming) limit that several server owners say leaves "virtually no room" for newer DLC content without dropping back to an older build. This is a genuine trade-off, not just an upgrade nag, worth testing on a staging copy before flipping NORTHLANDS across, and worth checking now whether NORTHLANDS is already tracking canary or sat on an older stable branch that will need an explicit `sv_enforceGameBuild` setting to avoid drift.

### 2. NoPixel confirms 5.0 will run natively through the Rockstar Games Launcher
**Source:** RockstarINTEL / Cfx tag sweep | https://rockstarintel.com/nopixel-5-0-announced-with-huge-gta-rp-changes/

Reported 13-14 August and still working through the FiveM community this week, NoPixel — the highest-profile GTA roleplay server — has confirmed its version 5.0 relaunch will be reachable directly through the Rockstar Games Launcher rather than solely through third-party FiveM client tooling. Closed beta is set for 8 September, with a trailer promised for 1 September, and invite-based access for testers. This is the first time a major RP server has been folded into Rockstar's own launcher rather than sitting alongside it, a direct consequence of Cfx.re (FiveM/RedM's maker) being owned by Rockstar since 2023 and the Cfx Marketplace going live in January this year.

For the FiveM ecosystem generally this is a signal of where Rockstar wants roleplay servers to sit: inside the official client, not bolted onto it. It does not change anything for NORTHLANDS today, but it is the clearest marker yet that Rockstar sees roleplay as a first-party feature going into GTA 6 rather than a tolerated mod scene, and it raises the long-run question of how independent servers get discovered once launcher-native listings exist.

### 3. Sins of a Solar Empire II reveals the Eidolon, its fourth faction, ahead of the Harbinger expansion
**Source:** Neowin | https://www.neowin.net/news/sins-of-a-solar-empire-ii-harbinger-expansion-reveals-new-eidolon-faction/

The Harbinger expansion's release date (3 September) was already tracked as an open loop; what's new this week is the design detail on its faction. The Eidolon are the ancient threat the Vasari have been fleeing for roughly ten thousand years in the game's lore, and Stardock has now detailed how they play: little interest in territory or economy, instead relying on infiltration, cloaked fleets, worlds concealed under corruption, and psionic abilities that can turn enemy crews on their own allies. Zero-class weapons that bypass shields entirely round out the kit.

This is a genuinely different faction archetype for the 4X/grand-strategy hybrid space — an asymmetric, economy-light spoiler faction rather than another builder civilisation — and it is the kind of design choice worth watching if Orwell ever looks at strategy-genre content or community angles again. Confirms Harbinger arrives 3 September at $29.99 standalone, bundled into the $99.99 Premium Edition or $59.99 Content Pass.

### 4. Company of Heroes: Definitive Edition narrows from "Fall 2026" to a September window
**Source:** GameWatcher | https://www.gamewatcher.com/company-of-heroes-definitive-edition-release-date-confirmed-september-2026-platforms-pre-orders-and-trailers

Relic's remaster, previously only pinned to "Fall 2026" and parked as a Gamescom watch item, has firmed up to a September 2026 window this week. The Definitive Edition bundles the original campaign with the Opposing Fronts and Tales of Valor expansions (three campaigns, four armies, 50-plus multiplayer/skirmish maps) behind a 64-bit engine upgrade, rebuilt lighting and water, a new "Ruthless" AI difficulty, and full ultrawide/grid-hotkey support. It was announced at June's PC Gaming Show rather than Gamescom, but a September launch means it will likely get further screen time during Gamescom week itself.

### 5. Gamescom week locks in: record sellout, dev conference opens Monday
**Source:** TechTimes / GamesRadar (search sweep) | https://www.techtimes.com/articles/324874/20260818/gamescom-2026-first-ever-sold-out-floor-full-schedule-where-watch.htm

Confirms the schedule the RTS/wargame drought watch has been waiting on. Gamescom's full 233,000 square metre show floor is sold out for the first time in the event's 18 editions, with 1,600-plus exhibitors. The developer conference "gamescom dev" runs 23-25 August, Geoff Keighley's Opening Night Live lands 25 August, and the public show floor opens 26-30 August in Cologne. Xbox, Nintendo, EA, Ubisoft, Capcom, SEGA, Bandai Namco, Konami, CD Projekt Red, Tencent and HoYoverse are all confirmed exhibitors. If the RTS/grand strategy/wargame release drought (day 26 today) is going to break, this is the week it happens — expect the next report or two to carry the actual announcements rather than just the calendar.
