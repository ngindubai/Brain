## Stories

### 1. Repterra hits 1.0 — dinosaur base-building survival RTS, published by MicroProse

**Source:** Saving Content | https://www.savingcontent.com/2026/08/06/base-building-dinosaur-survival-rts-repterra-charges-toward-1-0-release-on-august-20th/

Repterra left early access and launched into full 1.0 today. It's a base-building survival RTS: rebuild civilisation in a world overrun by dinosaurs, run colonies and economies, tame and breed dinosaurs, and fight hero-led battles claimed to render 100,000+ dinosaurs on screen. New at 1.0: a third faction, a Volcano biome added to the procedural map generator, expanded hero genetic-mutation customisation, and a custom level editor with Steam Workshop support. MicroProse is publishing.

This sits in the secondary bracket of today's brief — survival/base-building rather than grand strategy or wargame — but it's a genuine today-dated 1.0 launch in a genre segment that's been almost entirely silent for over three weeks. Worth a look if Orwell ever explores base-building-adjacent content or community angles, though there's no direct crossover with the FiveM/NORTHLANDS side of the portfolio.

### 2. GTA 6 leak saga: Cyberleek drops more footage, then gets disowned by Stop Killing Games

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/stop-killing-games-distances-itself-from-gta-6-leak-manifesto-using-illegal-means-to-make-a-point-is-unacceptable-to-us/ and Kotaku | https://kotaku.com/gta-6-leaks-stop-killing-games-skg-cyberleek-meme-coin-2000725806

Two developments since yesterday's report. First, Cyberleek made good on the threat to keep leaking: new footage dropped showing Jason driving at night, fighting security guards, tasering an NPC, and playing basketball, plus new map-location stills, with a line in the clip taunting Rockstar directly about VPN takedown attempts. Cyberleek also published a manifesto framing the leaks as consumer-rights activism against digital pre-orders, echoing Stop Killing Games' language.

Second and more consequential: Stop Killing Games publicly disowned the connection within about 24 hours, telling followers not to engage and calling the leaks "destructive." Its stated reason matters — SKG says Cyberleek has been attaching memecoin crypto links and QR codes to the leaked clips, asking viewers to pay for access to more, which SKG says undercuts any claim this is principled activism rather than a monetised leak campaign. That's a real reputational problem for Cyberleek's framing, not just noise. Rockstar has still made no official statement. Separately, leaked footage is also fuelling a fan debate over an apparent fuel/gas-gauge mechanic on vehicle HUDs — unconfirmed, cosmetic to the wider story, but adding to pre-Netflix-reveal noise ahead of 27 August.

### 3. EA's Saudi PIF takeover starts biting — Battlefield Studios layoffs, staff anxiety

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/ea-employees-brace-for-the-worst-under-saudi-ownership-hard-to-feel-good-about-being-bought-out-by-a-regime-thats-very-much-at-odds-with-progressive-society/

The $55bn PIF/Silver Lake/Affinity Partners acquisition of EA closed 4 August. Reporting out yesterday evening shows the fallout landing now: cuts have hit customer service, safety, IT and recruitment teams, escalating into layoffs at Battlefield Studios — the team behind the recently successful Battlefield 6. Staff across EA's Stockholm, Edmonton, LA and Guildford studios describe the ownership change moving from theoretical to real. Not an FiveM/RTS story, but worth a line for portfolio awareness: EA is a major industry employer and any further restructuring could shake loose talent or IP moves worth tracking.

### 4. Modern Warfare 4 beta pre-load opens; CoD NEXT lands tomorrow

**Source:** Call of Duty official blog | https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2026/08/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-next-early-intel-pc-specs

Pre-loading for the Modern Warfare 4 Early Access Beta opened today, 20 August, 9am PT, on all platforms bar Switch 2. CoD NEXT airs live tomorrow, 21 August, 9am PT, followed immediately by the Early Access Beta launch at 11am PT (runs 21–25 Aug for pre-order holders). Open Beta follows 28 Aug–1 Sept for everyone including Switch 2. Notable for the first time: Multiplayer, a campaign mission, and Warzone are all playable within the beta windows. Full release is 23 October. No direct NORTHLANDS relevance — this is Activision's engine, not Rockstar's — but it's a data point on the volume of live-service competition landing in Q4 alongside GTA 6.
