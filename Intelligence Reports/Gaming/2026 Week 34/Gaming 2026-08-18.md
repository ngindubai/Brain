## 1. BeastLink launches into Steam Early Access

**Source:** Gematsu | https://www.gematsu.com/2026/07/beastlink-launches-in-early-access-for-pc-on-august-18 ; Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/2962780/BeastLink/
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Grove Street Games — the Gainesville studio behind the GTA Trilogy: The Definitive Edition and the Switch port of ARK: Survival Ascended — put BeastLink into Steam Early Access today at $19.99. It's a 32-player match-based brawler: players fight as humans, drive vehicles, or "link" with one of four dormant kaiju (Horned Lizard, Mandrill, Bull Shark, Vampire Bat) to tear apart fully destructible cities, built on a proprietary "SuperDestruction" system — over 250,000 destructible objects per map, tens of millions of smaller physics pieces, networked so destruction persists and reshapes the map mid-match.

Early Access ships with three maps, four Beasts, two modes (Convergence, Battle Arena) and an initial slice of single-player missions. Console versions are confirmed but withheld until 1.0, pencilled for Summer 2027. It's closer to a multiplayer action game than the survival/base-building core of this beat, but the resource-scavenging-to-city-levelling loop and the studio's ARK pedigree put it close enough to flag. Worth a week-two look at concurrent player counts once the Early Access honeymoon settles — 32-player kaiju brawls are an unproven format at this budget tier.

## 2. Stackmon lands as a creature-collector/base-builder hybrid

**Source:** GameGrin | https://www.gamegrin.com/news/stackmon-announces-release-date-watch-the-trailer ; Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/3729550/Stackmon/
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Riftpoint Entertainment (published by Alibi Games) released Stackmon on Steam today: a creature-collector with a card-stacking resource layer, 100+ collectible Stackmon, 7 biomes and 25+ craftable/upgradeable buildings. This is a genuine stretch for the primary beat — it's a monster-taming game first — but it's the second Early Access/1.0 launch landing on the same calendar day as BeastLink, and the building/crafting layer gives it a loose base-building hook. Pre-launch demo reception ran 96% positive across a small sample (32 reviews). Flagged for completeness rather than significance; won't move the needle for NORTHLANDS or the wargame audience.
