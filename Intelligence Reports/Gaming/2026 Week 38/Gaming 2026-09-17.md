## Story 1: Endless Legend 2 leaves early access, lands on console for the first time
**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/strategy/endless-legend-2-review/ ; Amplitude Studios community blog | https://community.amplitude-studios.com/amplitude-studios/endless-legend-2/blogs/1070-endless-legend-2-releases-into-1-0-on-september-17
Endless Legend 2 hit version 1.0 today on PC (Steam, Epic, Microsoft Store) and, for the first time in the franchise, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The 1.0 build adds a sixth major faction, the Sandshapers (terraforming specialists working to return the world to its ancient desert state), plus two new rogue factions and the community-designed Mangrove of Harmony minor faction. A year of early access went into AI behaviour, faction balance, UI/UX and combat polish on top of the original build.

Reception is split by platform. PC Gamer's review is strongly positive, calling it a clear example of why Amplitude is loved by 4X fans, and Steam user reviews are sitting at 80-82% positive from a couple of thousand ratings. Console is a different story: 3.62/5 on PlayStation Store (84 ratings) and a flat 3/5 on Xbox Store (42 ratings), suggesting either a rougher console port or a slower-to-convert audience. For a 4X launch of this size, expect it to move Steam's strategy charts for a week or two; the console numbers are thin enough that they could shift either way as more ratings come in.

## Story 2: OpenFront and The Guild 1 Remake both launch into Early Access the same day
**Source:** Steam News (OpenFront) | https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3560670/view/701026055100563985 ; Gematsu (The Guild 1 Remake) | https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/the-guild-remake-europa-1410-early-access-roadmap-announced
Two strategy titles entered Early Access today. OpenFront, first shown at Gamescom, is a PvP territorial-conquest game: hundreds of players on one map forging alliances, building economies and using nuclear strikes to eliminate rivals. The Steam version adds exclusive cosmetics, a free month of premium, offline play and achievements over the browser original; developers expect a roughly six-month Early Access run.

The Guild 1 Remake: Europa 1410 (THQ Nordic/Ashborne Games) also launched into Early Access today, renamed from "The Guild – Europa 1410" after community pushback that the original title implied a Guild 2/3-style direction rather than a faithful remake of the 2002 original. It ships with six historical cities (including Graz, Nuremberg and Wrocław), eight professions and a dynasty-management economic sim core, and has banked over 300,000 wishlists ahead of launch. Neither title is a base-building/survival release in the strict sense the brief tracks, but both sit squarely in the strategy/economic-sim lane and both chose today deliberately, likely to ride Endless Legend 2's traffic rather than compete with it.

## Story 4: Steam Frame reservation lottery closes
**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/vr-hardware/steam-frame-starts-at-usd1-059-with-reservation-lottery-open-now-enter-before-september-17-for-best-chance-of-getting-one-this-year/ ; web search substitute for same-day closure reporting
The Steam Frame reservation window closed today at 10am Pacific as scheduled. Everyone who signed up during the open window (14-17 September) goes into the same randomised draw regardless of when they entered; Valve emails confirmed reservations or waitlist positions today, with purchase windows opening from tomorrow, 18 September. Pricing lands at $1,059 for the 256GB unit and $1,299 for 1TB, above Valve's own stated under-$1,000 target, which Valve has attributed to a memory/RAM component shortage. Not a NORTHLANDS or portfolio story, but the biggest PC hardware story of the week and worth tracking if any future venture touches VR or Steam hardware content.

## Story 5: FiveM — Kortz Center Heist support deadline surfaces, no new changelog today
**Source:** Cfx Forum (direct fetch) | https://forum.cfx.re/t/support-for-gta-online-the-kortz-center-heist-update-is-available-now/5422124 ; GitHub (qbox-project/qbx_core releases) | https://github.com/Qbox-project/qbx_core/releases
Nothing new shipped for FiveM or QBox today: qbx_core's last confirmed release is still v1.24.0 from 22 August, and there is no forum changelog or r/FiveM activity dated today. While checking sources, though, a dated forum post (20 August, re-surfaced in today's search results) confirmed a hard compatibility deadline that has not previously appeared in this brief: FXServer builds older than 35245 will stop being able to connect entirely after 15 October 2026, as part of the Kortz Center Heist title-update support rollout. The `sv_replaceExeToSwitchBuilds` parameter is now deprecated; servers must instead use `sv_enforceGameBuild`. Community forum threads flag unresolved issues with symlink handling, network thread performance and `.ymt` asset limits on the new build. This is not a today-story in the sense of new activity, but it is a genuine operational deadline worth a line for anyone running a FiveM server.
