## Story 1: Stronghold 4 gets a new trailer and confirmed early access shape at Tokyo Direct

**Source:** Windows Central / Gematsu, via web search | https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/pc-gaming-show-tokyo-direct-2026-here-are-its-watch-times-streaming-platforms-and-what-games-you-can-expect-to-see, https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/pc-gaming-show-tokyo-direct-set-for-september-20

Firefly Studios' Stronghold 4 got a new trailer during today's 90-minute PC Gaming Show Tokyo Direct broadcast (5pm BST). The castle-sim prequel, following Penryn of Wethel, has topped 500k wishlists since its June reveal and a September 4 story trailer. Today's showing reiterated the early access plan: a 22-mission story campaign, Skirmish Trail, Free Build and Custom Skirmish at launch, with online multiplayer, an economic campaign and co-op added later across an expected six-month early access run. Still no firm early access date beyond "2026."

## Story 2: Tokyo Direct's wider slate touches base-building and survival horror

**Source:** Gematsu / web search | https://www.gematsu.com/2026/09/pc-gaming-show-tokyo-direct-set-for-september-20

Beyond Stronghold 4, the Direct carried a deep dive into Tenebris Somnia (New Blood Interactive's retro survival horror adventure with live-action cutscenes), a new look at Springloaded's management/building sim Let's Build a Dungeon, and an exclusive release-date reveal for Devolver Digital's Virtue and a Sledgehammer. Total slate: 35+ games, over 10 world premieres, publishers including Atari, Devolver Digital, Firefly Studios and New Blood Interactive. Nothing else on the list confirmed as RTS, grand strategy or wargame beyond Stronghold 4.

## Story 3: Tokyo Game Show cuts its run short, typhoon forces first-ever mid-show cancellation

**Source:** BoxThisLap, via web search | https://boxthislap.org/tokyo-game-show-2026-final-day-cancelled/

Organiser CESA cancelled tomorrow's Monday session (21 September) on 19 September, citing Typhoon No. 25 bringing stronger wind and rain from Monday afternoon and risk to public transport. That makes today, Sunday, the actual final physical day of TGS 2026's public run, and CESA said it is the first time in the show's 30-year history a day has been cancelled while the event was already under way. Tickets for the cancelled day are being refunded; some stage content may move online. This directly shortens the window strategy exhibitors (Koei Tecmo's Nobunaga's Ambition and others, covered in yesterday's report) have on the floor.

## Story 4: Koei Tecmo teases new Romance of the Three Kingdoms project at 40th anniversary stage

**Source:** Noisy Pixel / web search | https://noisypixel.net/koei-tecmo-tgs-2026-lineup-attack-on-titan-3-wo-long-2/

Koei Tecmo's TGS stage today included a segment marking 40 years of Romance of the Three Kingdoms, its long-running grand strategy series. Franchise founder Kou Shibusawa closed the segment with a retrospective on how RTK has changed across four decades, then teased a still-unannounced new project tied to the anniversary. No further detail given. Worth tracking given RTK's direct lineage into the grand strategy genre this brief covers.

## Story 5: Japan Game Awards Future Division ceremony held today, honouring unreleased TGS titles

**Source:** Japan Game Awards official site / web search | https://awards.cesa.or.jp/en/future/overview/

The Future Division ceremony, which recognises unreleased games exhibited at TGS through public vote, ran at 1pm today on the TGS Event Stage. Voting covered any unreleased title shown at TGS 2026 (17–21 September) up to 4pm on 19 September, so this year's typhoon-shortened final day did not affect eligibility. Results were not yet published at time of writing; open loop to confirm whether any strategy or survival title placed.
