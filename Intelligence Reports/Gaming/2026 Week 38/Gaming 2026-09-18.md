## Story 1: PAX Australia reveals its October showcase lineup — Total War: Warhammer 40,000 among the playables

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/ (PAX Australia showcase article, 18 September)

PAX Australia confirmed today that Total War: Warhammer 40,000, Alien: Isolation 2 and Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 will be playable on the show floor when the Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre event runs 9-11 October. This is the first confirmation of Total War 40K as a playable build outside a controlled SEGA/TGS demo booth, which matters for how far along the campaign and battle systems actually are — a public-facing playable build at a consumer show is a different confidence signal than a curated press demo. No new release-date information came with it; the game remains pointed at the 2026 holiday season per prior reporting.

## Story 2: Tokyo Game Show's trade-only second day — Persona 4 Revival trailer, Total War 40K confirmed for its first PlayStation entry, and a UAE studio on the show floor

**Source:** Siliconera | https://www.siliconera.com/tgs-2026-persona-4-revival-trailer-focuses-on-naoto-shirogane/ | The National | https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/pop-culture/2026/09/17/tokyo-game-show-2026-offers-glimpse-of-gamings-next-chapter-with-uae-in-the-mix/

TGS 2026's second day (18 September) was business-only, so there was no public-facing reveal slate; the day's confirmed content is a new Persona 4 Revival trailer introducing the final playable character, Naoto Shirogane, ahead of the game's 18 February 2027 release. More relevant to this brief's genre focus: SEGA's TGS presence confirms Total War: Warhammer 40,000 is heading to PlayStation, the series' first console entry ever, alongside its existing Xbox/PC plan. Separately, Abu Dhabi's Red Dunes Games brought nine titles to TGS this year — Zozo and the Lost Dreams, Port of Jumanah and Nightmare Circus among them — the UAE's most expanded presence at the show to date, part of a 759-exhibitor, 51-country, five-day edition (its largest ever, running to 21 September). None of the UAE titles are in this brief's primary genre lens, but the presence itself is a small, genuine data point on the UAE's growing gaming-industry footprint, adjacent to the wider portfolio's Gulf focus.

## Story 3: October's release schedule gets crowded — Valor Mortis prices itself as an "and game" rather than a competitor

**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/ (October release schedule article, 18 September)

Valor Mortis, a first-person soulslike, is set to launch 13 October into a month already stacked with bigger releases. Its publisher has priced it at $40 specifically so it functions as an impulse "and game" — something bought alongside a bigger release rather than competed against directly — a deliberate positioning choice rather than a genre release. It sits outside this brief's core RTS/wargame/survival lens, but the pricing strategy itself is a useful read on how smaller studios are choosing to navigate an increasingly stacked release calendar; worth a one-line note rather than full coverage given the genre mismatch.
