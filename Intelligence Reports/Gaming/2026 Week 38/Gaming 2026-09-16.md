## Story 1: Steam Frame reservation lottery enters its final day
**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/vr-hardware/steam-frame-starts-at-usd1-059-with-reservation-lottery-open-now-enter-before-september-17-for-best-chance-of-getting-one-this-year/ ; Tom's Hardware | https://www.tomshardware.com/virtual-reality/valve-engineers-discuss-the-duality-of-the-steam-frame-and-pricing-valves-newest-vr-headset-pivots-steamos-to-arm
The Steam Frame lottery (open since 14 September) closes tomorrow, 17 September at 10am Pacific. Signups get shuffled once the window shuts; there is no advantage to having entered early. Valve has been explicit that it wanted to land under $1,000 and blames a memory/RAM component crisis for the $1,059-$1,299 pricing that shipped instead. Reviews out this week are consistent: comfortable, strong as a streaming box for an existing PC library, weak battery life (roughly two hours), and a resale/scalping risk that Valve is trying to blunt with the reservation-and-purchase-window system rather than an open sale. Nothing new broke today specifically; this is the last full day of the entry window, so it is the natural checkpoint. Not a NORTHLANDS-relevant story, but it is the biggest PC hardware story running through the day and worth a glance if any of the portfolio ever touches VR or Steam hardware content.
