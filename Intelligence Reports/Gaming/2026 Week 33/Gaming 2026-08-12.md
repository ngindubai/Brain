## 1. Mandate Order launches into Steam and Epic Early Access
**Source:** Games Press / Bitvoxy | https://www.bitvoxy.com/historical-strategy-game-mandate-order-launches-on-steam-and-epic-games-store-on-august-12
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Digital Sky's ancient Chinese war city-builder went live in Early Access today at $19.99 (15% launch discount for two weeks), on both Steam and Epic simultaneously. It pairs freeform city construction, agriculture and logistics chains with population management, retainer diplomacy drawn from the Hundred Schools of Thought, conscription and large-scale real-time battles — closer to Manor Lords with a war layer bolted on than a pure RTS. Close to 100,000 wishlists went in ahead of launch, across 12 supported languages. The studio has set expectations for roughly twelve months in Early Access before a 1.0, explicitly tying that timeline to player feedback rather than a fixed date. Early Steam reviews weren't populated in enough volume yet at time of writing to call reception either way — worth a look again tomorrow.

The genre-fit question matters for the drought thread below: Mandate Order has RTS-scale battles but ships as a city-builder first. It's a release the starved strategy audience will point to, but it's not the pure RTS or wargame release that would cleanly close out the drought.

## 3. Helldivers 2 x Warhammer 40,000 Legendary Warbond goes live, alongside a new Illuminate enemy tease
**Source:** PC Gamer | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/third-person-shooter/the-helldivers-2-x-warhammer-40k-warbond-arrives-on-august-12-bringing-three-of-its-most-iconic-weapons-and-two-imperial-guard-armour-sets/
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Arrowhead's Castellan's Creed Legendary Warbond dropped today, built around Cadian Kasrkin-inspired gear: the TG-122 Demo-Trooper heavy armour for fortification-breaching and the TG-8 Sharpshooter medium set, both carrying a True Grit passive for weapon handling and reload speed, plus capes, a vehicle pattern and a player title, unlocked for 1,500 Super Credits. Alongside the Warbond, Arrowhead's accompanying title update teases a new Illuminate enemy type and continues the weapon-balance pass flagged in earlier patch notes. Outside the core RTS/wargame lane, but the Warhammer 40K license crossover is a genuine wargaming-audience story — Games Workshop IP landing in a live-service shooter is the kind of crossover that pulls tabletop wargamers into PC gaming spend, which is the closest adjacent signal to today's strategy drought.
