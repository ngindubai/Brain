## 1. Disney Declares Kingdom Hearts a Billion-Dollar Franchise at 25th Anniversary Panel
**Source:** Push Square / PC Gamer | https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/08/on-the-cusp-of-kingdom-hearts-4-blowout-disney-says-series-is-a-billion-dollar-brand | https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/disney-says-that-kingdom-hearts-is-now-a-billion-dollar-franchise/
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Disney hosted a livestreamed Kingdom Hearts panel today (4:30pm PT) marking the series' 25th anniversary, using the moment to confirm nine mainline games have each grossed over $1bn at retail — the first time Disney has put a formal "billion-dollar franchise" label on the Square Enix/Disney RPG crossover. The panel promised a behind-the-scenes look at the franchise's development history rather than new-game news specifically, though the timing (anniversary plus fresh commercial framing) reads as scene-setting ahead of further Kingdom Hearts IV material.

Not RTS/wargame-adjacent, but a genuine today-dated commercial milestone worth noting as a data point on how durable a 25-year action-RPG franchise's retail economics still are against the current live-service-dominated market.

## 2. Modern Warfare 4's Beta Structure Confirmed: Campaign, Multiplayer and Warzone in One Test
**Source:** Insider Gaming / CGMagOnline / GameSpot | https://insider-gaming.com/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4-beta-includes-campaign-multiplayer-warzone/
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Infinity Ward's beta plan for Modern Warfare 4, announced yesterday and still the freshest angle in today's coverage, runs pre-order early access from 21-25 August followed by an open beta from 28 August-1 September. Unusually for a Call of Duty beta, it bundles all three components: the campaign mission "Entrenched" (a first for a CoD beta), seven multiplayer maps including the 500-configuration "Kill Block," and the franchise's first-ever official Warzone beta on a new Zodiac Resurgence map.

Included as a one-day-old story still generating today's discussion rather than being re-announced; not RTS/wargame-adjacent but a genuine breadth-relevant data point on how the shooter genre's biggest franchise is testing ahead of launch.
