## Story 1: Kingdom Rush 6: Genesis TD launches on Steam and mobile

**Source:** Ironhide Game Studio | https://www.ironhidegames.com/News/Details/482 (also: Steam | https://store.steampowered.com/app/4259190/Kingdom_Rush_6_Genesis_TD/)

Ironhide's sixth mainline Kingdom Rush title launched today on Steam, App Store and Google Play at $6.99. It's a prequel: the time-travel framing sends series mainstay Gerald Lightseeker back to the founding days of Linirea, tasking players with recruiting the heroes who built the kingdom and stopping a younger Vez'nan across 18 stages. A four-stage Steam demo has been live ahead of launch, and mobile pre-orders were open in advance.

Kingdom Rush is the closest thing tower defence has to a reliable franchise anchor, and Ironhide has kept a tight scope (single studio, $6.99 price, mobile-first economics ported cleanly to PC) that consistently earns back its budget. Nothing here changes the competitive landscape, but it's a useful data point on how a small strategy studio keeps a low-cost franchise profitable release after release. There is no NORTHLANDS or FiveM angle; pure genre-watch item.

## Story 2: Dragon Shelter launches into the crowded farming/building-sim lane

**Source:** GameSpot | https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-biggest-new-game-releases-of-september-2026/

Dragon Shelter, a fantasy farming/building sim in which players restore a broken alliance between humans and dragons while managing a farm, caring for creatures, exploring and cooking, released today. It sits in the crafting/base-management lane adjacent to survival and base-building rather than squarely inside it, and coverage so far is limited to release-calendar listings rather than reviews.

Worth flagging only as a genre-adjacent data point: the cosy-farm-sim-with-building-systems subgenre keeps shipping at pace, and it remains one of the more reliable indie categories for sustained post-launch engagement. No operational read-across to NORTHLANDS.
