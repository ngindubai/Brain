## Story 1: Tokyo Game Show 2026 closes at 257,642 attendees, exhibitors split on the cancelled final day

**Source:** GamesBeat, via web search | https://gamesbeat.com/tokyo-game-show-exhibitors-express-relief-and-regret-over-sept-21-typhoon-cancellation/

With today's scheduled fifth day cancelled for Typhoon No. 25 (covered yesterday), coverage today confirmed TGS 2026's final tally: 257,642 total attendees across its four live days, short of both the 2024 and 2025 totals despite year-on-year growth on the opening business day (17 September) and the first public day (19 September). Exhibitor reaction was mixed rather than uniformly negative: some described relief at an unplanned rest day after four straight days of demos and meetings, others regret at losing a final day of business contacts, and at least three reported difficulty booking trucks to clear stands early. No strategy, wargame or survival title featured in the reactions reported. This closes out the TGS thread; no further TGS-specific items expected in tomorrow's report unless CESA issues a formal post-mortem.

## Story 2: Japan Game Awards Future Division results published

**Source:** GoNintendo / Persona Central, via web search | https://gonintendo.com/contents/65242-japan-game-awards-2026-s-future-award-winners-revealed, https://personacentral.com/p4r-future-division/

Yesterday's report flagged the Future Division ceremony as held but unresolved. Results are now public: 10 titles were honoured from 537 eligible unreleased games exhibited at TGS, chosen by public vote taken 17–19 September. Named winners include Persona 4 Revival, Final Fantasy VII Revelation and Romancing SaGa 3: Destinies Unite. No RTS, grand strategy, wargame or survival/base-building title placed among the 10; this is a JRPG-heavy result and has no read-across to the genres this brief tracks. Open loop resolved.
