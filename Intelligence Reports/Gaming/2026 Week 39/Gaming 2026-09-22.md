## Story 1: Dune: Awakening hits full console release with single-player mode

**Source:** Xbox Wire / Dune: Awakening official | https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/06/04/dune-awakening-xbox-release-date-details/

Funcom's open-world survival MMO goes fully live on PS5 and Xbox Series today, landing on Xbox Game Pass day one. Deluxe/Ultimate owners got a head start on the 17th; today is the wide console release for everyone else.

The bigger change is the new single-player mode: full offline play through all five acts of "Book One," with manual tuning for resource rates, sandworm spawns, sandstorms and death penalties. Character progression is now saved per character rather than per server/shard, so a character can move between worlds freely. Also in: full controller support on PC, Steam Deck verification, GeForce Now support, and a rebuilt Landsraad running as a mission-based framework instead of the old faction-point grind.

This is a genre-relevant, well-resourced open-world survival title going mainstream today, worth tracking as a benchmark for base-building/survival UX and single-player-mode monetisation approaches.

## Story 2: Delverium launches into Steam Early Access

**Source:** Steam / Gamers Heroes | https://store.steampowered.com/app/2710040/Delverium/

Delverium (open-world co-op survival/crafting/base-building for up to 8 players) went live in Early Access today at a 20% launch discount ($9.99, rising to $12.49 after 5 October). Steam Deck Verified. Early reception is strong: 100% positive across its first user reviews, off the back of 80,000+ wishlists pre-launch.

This closes yesterday's open loop; it shipped on schedule. Genre-fit is exact for the primary sweep (survival, open-world, base-building), and it's a useful comparison point for scale (8-player co-op cap, procedural world).
