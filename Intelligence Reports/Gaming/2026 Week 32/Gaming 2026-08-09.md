## 1. QuakeCon 2026 closes today, thirty years on and half the studio gone

QuakeCon 2026 runs its final day today at the Gaylord Texan in Grapevine, Texas, closing out the four-day, thirtieth-anniversary BYOC event. The convention has been a split affair: id Software's four original co-founders (John Romero, John Carmack, Adrian Carmack, Tom Hall) shared a stage together for the first time since 1993, and MachineGames shipped a free 19-map Quake campaign, "Dawn of the Machine," on day one. Set against that is Microsoft's Xbox division cutting roughly 136 id Software staff in July, about half the studio, as part of a wider 3,200-role Xbox gaming cut. John Carmack said publicly the layoffs would "dampen the mood" of the founders' reunion, and id Software has confirmed headcount is now back to what it was for Doom (2016). Today's closing sessions and BYOC tournament finals wrap the event; no separate closing-day announcement has surfaced beyond the standing schedule.

## 2. GTA 3D trilogy fan-patch ships its biggest update yet

SilentPatch, the long-running community fix project for the GTA III/Vice City/San Andreas remastered trilogy, released its largest update to date today: over 100 fixes, including restored voice dialogue in San Andreas that had been missing since the 2021 Definitive Edition relaunch. This is fan tooling, not an official Rockstar patch, and it is not FiveM-related, but it is a live signal that the modding ecosystem around Rockstar's back catalogue is still active and well-resourced heading into the GTA VI run-up.

## 4. Manor Lords 0.8.097 beta unchanged, but the beta content is worth logging properly
