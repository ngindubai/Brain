## 1. QuakeCon 2026 opens — id Software's four founders reunite for the first time since 1993
**Source:** GamingBolt (via search) | https://gamingbolt.com/quakecon-2026-celebrates-30-years-of-quake-from-august-6th-to-9th ; Plus Forward (via search) | https://www.plusforward.net/post/91843/QuakeCon-2026-set-for-August-6-9/

QuakeCon 2026 runs today through Sunday (6-9 August) in Grapevine, Texas, marking Quake's 30th anniversary. The headline draw is a reunion panel of id Software's four founders — John Carmack, Adrian Carmack, John Romero and Tom Hall — discussing the studio's origins from Commander Keen through Wolfenstein 3D, DOOM and Quake; they haven't shared a stage together since 1993. The event lands weeks after Xbox layoffs hit parts of the wider studio group, and id Software used the run-up to confirm it still has "the crew we need to build the games and tech we're known for." Rumour mill has a Wolfenstein (2009) remaster announcement in play, unconfirmed.

Not a primary-lane genre match and no NORTHLANDS relevance, but the clearest gaming-industry story of the day and worth flagging as a signal that id's identity is intact post-layoffs.
