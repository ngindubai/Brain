## 1. GTA VI: Rockstar confirms Netflix "Extended Look" for 27 August; Take-Two's Q1 earnings call happened today but results unconfirmed as of research time
**Source:** Rockstar Games via X | https://x.com/RockstarGames/status/2085335127287030232 ; HotHardware | https://hothardware.com/news/gta-6-extended-look-netflix-ahead-game-launch ; PSU | https://www.psu.com/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-an-extended-look-confirmed-for-netflix-on-august-27/
**Related vault notes:** [[Gaming]]

Rockstar has confirmed, via its own account, that "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" premieres on Netflix on Thursday 27 August at 3pm ET, then hits the official Rockstar YouTube channel and the GTA VI website six hours later at 9pm ET. This is a genuine break from Rockstar's usual marketing playbook — routing a major reveal through a streaming platform rather than a straight trailer drop — and lands three months ahead of the 19 November launch. Early reporting frames it as more than a trailer: expectations are for actual gameplay mechanics and possibly mission footage, not just cinematics. This directly resolves yesterday's open loop: Henderson's speculative 6 August date came and went with nothing, but Rockstar filled the silence with a firm, official date instead.

Separately, Take-Two's fiscal Q1 2027 earnings call ran today at 8am ET, its first financial window into GTA VI pre-order performance since the pre-order window opened 25 June. This is an unusual slot for Take-Two — first-ever pre-market release, first-ever Friday release, breaking a longstanding midweek pattern — which is itself being read as a signal that management wanted to control the narrative rather than let a Tuesday-Thursday habit dictate timing. As of research time today, no outlet had published confirmed actual results: Take-Two's own investor relations press release page still shows only the 9 July "save the date" notice, not a results release. Guidance going in was $1.45-1.50bn revenue and a loss of 15-23 cents per share, with fiscal 2027 net bookings guidance of $8.0-8.2bn already on the table from the May call. Treat today's actual print as unresolved — check first thing tomorrow.

No NORTHLANDS relevance directly, but the Netflix date is the one to calendar: once "Extended Look" airs on 27 August, expect a fresh wave of FiveM migration chatter and GTA VI asset speculation across the roleplay community.
