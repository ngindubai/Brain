## 1. EA's $55bn take-private closes today
**Source:** Multiple via search | https://www.pocketgamer.biz/ea-expects-acquisition-by-pif-led-consortium-to-close-on-august-4th/ ; https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/eas-55-billion-buyout-officially-212935263.html

Electronic Arts' leveraged buyout by a consortium of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (93.4%), Silver Lake (5.5%) and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners (1.1%) is set to close "on or about the close of trading" today, per EA's 30 July SEC filing confirming all regulatory approvals are in hand. Shareholders cash out at $210/share; the $55bn enterprise value breaks down to roughly $52.5bn equity plus assumed debt, funded by about $36bn equity and $20bn in JPMorgan-committed debt. Andrew Wilson stays as CEO. As of writing there's no market confirmation the deal has formally completed — Dubai is well ahead of US market close — so treat "closed" as expected-today rather than done-and-dusted until tomorrow's report can confirm the delisting.

This is the largest LBO in history and ends EA's public run at 35 years. No direct NORTHLANDS relevance, but it's the clearest marker yet of sovereign-wealth capital's appetite for AAA publishers — worth watching if PIF-adjacent money starts showing interest in the UAE gaming/esports space specifically.

## 2. GTA VI: Rockstar account maintenance window fuels Trailer 3 speculation
**Source:** GTABase (via search) / egw.news summary | https://x.com/GTABase/status/2084413569886568602

Rockstar's account-services backend went into maintenance today between 4:00–6:00 UTC, during which players couldn't modify account details, including deletion. GTABase flagged it as possible prep for new profile features tied to GTA VI, since several legacy GTA V account features have been sunset. Insiders NateTheHate and Tom Henderson have separately been pointing at August for news, with Henderson floating 6 August specifically for a trailer. None of this is official — Rockstar has said nothing — and it's exactly the kind of maintenance window that happens for mundane reasons too.

Genuinely new today-dated development, but speculative. Still no movement on the actual trailer, and Take-Two's 7 August earnings call remains the firmer date to watch. No direct NORTHLANDS relevance beyond the general FiveM-migration timeline once GTA VI content starts circulating.
