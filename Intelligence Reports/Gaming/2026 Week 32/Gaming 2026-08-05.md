## 1. EA's $55bn take-private formally confirmed closed
**Source:** The Wrap (via search) | https://www.thewrap.com/industry-news/deals-ma/electronic-arts-goes-private-55-billion-buyout-closes/ ; EA.com | https://www.ea.com/news/ea-announces-completion-of-acquisition

Electronic Arts confirmed today that its acquisition by a consortium of Saudi Arabia's PIF (93.4%), Silver Lake (5.5%) and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners (1.1%) has completed, closing out yesterday's "expected today, not yet confirmed" open loop. Shareholders received $210/share cash; EA has ceased NASDAQ trading and delisted. Andrew Wilson stays CEO, headquarters remain in Redwood City. This is the largest LBO in history. No direct NORTHLANDS relevance, but it closes the loop on the clearest marker yet of sovereign-wealth appetite for AAA publishers.
