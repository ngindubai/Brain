## Story 1: Taiwan logs a heavy PLA activity day as Han Kuang drills continue

**Source:** Taiwan Ministry of National Defense (via search) | https://www.globalsecurity.org/wmd/library/news/taiwan/2026/taiwan-260808-roc-mnd01.htm ; TaiwanPlus (via search) | https://www.taiwanplus.com/news/taiwan-news/military/250811008/analysis-surge-in-chinas-adiz-incursions-show-pla-air-forces-capacity

**Related vault notes:** [[Agent Org]]

Taiwan's MND detected 14 PLA aircraft sorties, 6 PLAN vessels and 8 other official ships operating around the island as of 6am local time today, with 11 of the 14 sorties crossing the Taiwan Strait median line into the island's northern and southwestern ADIZ. This sits within the elevated-but-routine range PLA activity has settled into this year rather than marking a new escalation, but it lands on the same day Taiwan's Han Kuang exercise continues (the second scheduled mobile-network blackout drill is still set for Thursday, per yesterday's report) — meaning both sides are running live posture-signalling simultaneously rather than either one being a response to the other.

Why it matters: this is the daily baseline that makes any future genuine escalation harder to spot early, and it's worth tracking the trendline rather than any single day's count. No incident, no diplomatic statement attached to today's figures specifically.

## Story 4: Congress moves a fresh Russia/Iran sanctions bill through the House — sanctions/export-controls thread

**Source:** Rep. Michael McCaul's office (press release, direct fetch) | https://mccaul.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/mccaul-hoyer-colleagues-introduce-lindsey-o-graham-sanctioning-russia

**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]], [[Markets]]

One day old rather than today-dated, but genuinely new and worth flagging on the sanctions/export-controls thread this section tracks: Reps. Michael McCaul (R-TX) and Steny Hoyer (D-MD) introduced the House companion to the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026 yesterday, with 18 bipartisan cosponsors including several committee chairs. The Senate passed its version 86-11 on 7 August. The bill targets buyers of Russian oil and gas, Russian financial institutions and shadow-fleet operators, plus extended Iran energy/weapons sanctions. The House is on recess until September, so an actual floor vote isn't expected before the fall — today's fact is simply that the House text now exists and cosponsors are signing on ahead of that vote.

Why it matters: this runs in parallel with the US's separately signed (30 July) critical-minerals export-restriction directive under the Defense Production Act — two distinct threads of the same broader trend toward tighter, more codified US economic-statecraft tools against Russia, Iran and (via the minerals directive) supply-chain competitors generally. Nothing here moves markets today, but it's the kind of bill that eventually does once a floor vote is scheduled.

## Story 5: Sudan's army-aligned Joint Force repels a second RSF push on Bir Saliba, West Darfur

**Source:** Sudan Tribune | https://sudantribune.com/article/317171 ; Al Jazeera | https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/10/has-the-sudanese-army-turned-the-page-on-control-of-western-sudan

**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

The Joint Force and Sudanese army repelled a two-pronged RSF assault on Bir Saliba, West Darfur — the second such attempt in under a week, with RSF fighters attacking from Kulbus in the north and Soleia in the south trying to encircle allied positions. This sits within a broader army-led counteroffensive that's produced a run of gains in western Sudan over recent days, though analysts quoted by The National caution that Khartoum's stated goal of fully defeating the RSF this year is "too ambitious." The IOM separately reported over 6,650 people displaced from 11 villages in the Sirba region as fighting has intensified.

Why it matters: a genuine shift-in-momentum story rather than a single-day event — worth watching whether the army's gains around El Geneina hold, since a durable reversal of RSF territorial control in West Darfur would be one of the war's few clear turning points this year. No direct Orwell read-through; flagged for regional-risk completeness and because Sudan's trajectory affects broader Horn of Africa and Red Sea stability.
