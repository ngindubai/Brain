## Story 1: CoreWeave beats on Q2, backlog surges past $104bn, guidance raised

**Source:** CNBC | https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/coreweave-crwv-q2-earnings-report-2026.html
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

CoreWeave reported Q2 results after the close on 11 August, and the market reaction is landing today. Adjusted EPS came in at a loss of $1.03 versus a loss of $1.20 expected. Revenue hit $2.58bn against $2.56bn expected, up 112% year-on-year. Net loss widened to $626m from $290m a year ago — the losses are real and growing, driven by the capex and financing cost of building out capacity ahead of demand. Shares jumped 13.16% in after-hours trading to $102.21.

The number that matters most: revenue backlog exceeding $104bn. For 2026 the company now guides to $960m-$1.15bn in adjusted operating income on $12.4bn-$13.2bn in revenue, with capex of $35bn-$39bn and active power capacity over 1.85 gigawatts by year end. New business with Anthropic and Meta closed in the quarter.

This is the clearest read yet on how the market is pricing GPU-cloud economics: investors are willing to look past a widening net loss when backlog visibility is this deep. It's the same logic underwriting Nvidia's $500bn financing push (still repricing, see below) — capital is flowing to anyone who can prove contracted demand for compute, loss-making or not. Worth tracking as a comp for any GPU compute venture thinking about neocloud-style capacity plays: CoreWeave's backlog-to-revenue ratio (roughly 8x forward annual revenue) is the bar the market is now using to judge credibility in this space.

## Story 3: Cisco reports Q4 FY26 after the bell — AI infrastructure orders the number to watch

**Source:** Alphastreet / TipRanks (preview) | https://news.alphastreet.com/cisco-systems-q4-2026-earnings-preview-august-12-street-expects-1-17-eps/amp/
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

Cisco reports fiscal Q4 2026 results after today's close. Street consensus: EPS of $1.17 (+18% YoY), revenue around $16.82bn-$16.83bn (+15% YoY). The stock is up roughly 46-61% year-to-date (estimates vary by source) on one story: hyperscaler AI infrastructure networking orders, which management has guided toward roughly $9bn for the fiscal year. Analysts are looking for networking revenue around $9.6bn, up 26% year-on-year and an acceleration from the prior quarter's 25% growth. Options markets are pricing an 8.26% move on the print.

This is a useful read-through independent of the GPU vendors themselves: Cisco sells the networking fabric that ties GPU clusters together, so its order book is a cleaner signal of actual hyperscaler capex intent than any single chip company's commentary, which is more exposed to allocation and mix noise. A strong print here corroborates the CoreWeave backlog story above — real capacity is still being ordered, not just financed. A miss or soft guide would be the first genuine crack in the "infrastructure demand is unlimited" narrative this week. Worth checking the print tonight; I'll cover the outcome in tomorrow's report.
