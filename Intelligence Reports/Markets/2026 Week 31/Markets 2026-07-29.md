## Story 1: SK Hynix posts record Q2 profit but misses consensus; Asia chip stocks rebound off yesterday's rout

**Source:** Korea Herald | https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10823621 ; Korea Times | https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/companies/20260729/sk-hynix-q2-profit-reaches-416-bil-up-557 ; CNBC (via search) | https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/29/sk-hynix-earnings-profit-revenue-hbm-memory.html ; KED Global | https://www.kedglobal.com/earnings/newsView/ked202607290001
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]], [[Comparison Network]]

SK Hynix reported operating profit of 60.54 trillion won ($41.6bn), up 557% year-on-year, on revenue of 79.32 trillion won (+256.8% YoY, +51% quarter-on-quarter) — a genuine record and the first time the company has cleared 100 trillion won in first-half revenue. Net profit hit 93.92 trillion won, an operating margin of 76% and net margin of 118%, driven by HBM, AI server DRAM and enterprise SSD pricing. The catch: results still missed brokerage consensus of 83.94 trillion won revenue and 63.99 trillion won operating profit, which is why this is a genuine earnings miss dressed as a record quarter.

The market read it as good news regardless: in early Asian trading today the Kospi rebounded, with SK Hynix up more than 3%, Samsung Electronics up over 4%, and Kioxia surging more than 7% — a sharp reversal from yesterday's 10-11% circuit-breaker plunge covered in yesterday's report. For the GPU compute venture this closes the loop on yesterday's volatility event: the underlying AI-memory demand story is intact and record-breaking, yesterday's rout reads more like a valuation-multiple correction than a demand problem. Whether the bounce holds through the rest of the week, particularly once Microsoft and Meta's capex guidance lands tonight (Story 3), is the next thing to watch.

## Story 2: FOMC decision due 2pm ET — Kevin Warsh's Fed widely expected to hold, but not unanimously

**Source:** Kotak Neo | https://www.kotakneo.com/news/global-markets/us-fed-meeting-interest-rate-decision-july-29/ ; CNBC (via search) | https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/warsh-fed-rate-decision-trump-powell-analysis.html ; Conference Board | https://www.conference-board.org/research/global-economy-briefs/rapid-response-fomc-preview-july-2026
**Related vault notes:** [[Markets]]

The Federal Reserve announces its rate decision at 2pm ET today, with Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference at 2:30pm. Consensus (roughly 62% of market participants per pre-meeting polling) expects a fifth consecutive hold at 3.50-3.75%, though a meaningful minority is pricing a hike to 3.75-4.00% given inflation running at 4.2% and Warsh's own hawkish leanings. Analysts note Warsh has three reasons to hold off on a hike this week specifically: renewed Iran-related energy-price uncertainty (Story in [[Geopolitics]]), still-cooling underlying inflation data, and the political optics of moving on his watch this early. Warsh has also signalled he'll give less forward guidance than his predecessor, so today's presser may resolve less about the September meeting than usual.

This is Warsh's first live rate decision under real market stress — yesterday's Kospi circuit breaker and last night's Iran missile interception both landed directly ahead of today's meeting. The CBUAE, which tracks the Fed via the dirham peg, holds its own rate at 3.65% and will move in lockstep with whatever the Fed decides. A hold is the base case and not itself news; a hike, or notably hawkish language in the presser, would be the story to flag same-day.

## Story 3: Microsoft and Meta report after today's close — the AI-capex earnings week begins in earnest

**Source:** TipRanks | https://www.tipranks.com/news/big-tech-earnings-showdown-meta-and-microsoft-in-focus-ahead-of-july-29-earnings-report ; 24/7 Wall St. | https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/28/earnings-showdown-is-microsoft-or-meta-more-at-risk-when-july-29-earnings-drop/ ; PrimeXBT | https://primexbt.com/news/microsoft-meta-apple-and-amazon-report-earnings-within-48-hours-on-july-29-and-3/
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

Microsoft reports fiscal Q4 2026 today, consensus EPS $4.22-4.24 on revenue of $87.5-87.7bn, call at 2:30pm PT. Meta reports Q2 fiscal 2026 the same day, consensus EPS $7.18-7.24, with its own call tomorrow. Both land less than 24 hours after Alphabet's own report, where Alphabet beat on headline numbers but its shares fell the next day after guiding 2026 capex to $195-200bn — a pattern the market is now primed to repeat on Microsoft and Meta if either raises AI infrastructure spending guidance without a matching demand story. Short interest in Magnificent 7 names has reportedly been building into this week's prints, per 24/7 Wall St., suggesting the market is positioned for disappointment rather than upside surprise.

Given yesterday's Corning and KLA reaction (Story 4) — both companies beat on headline numbers and were sold off hard on guidance and margin concerns — tonight's Microsoft and Meta capex commentary is the cleanest read yet on whether Wall Street's patience with AI infrastructure spending is genuinely thinning. Worth a same-day or next-morning follow-up once both calls conclude.

## Story 4: Corning and KLA both beat yesterday's earnings estimates — and were both sold off anyway

**Source:** Yahoo Finance | https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/corning-q2-2026-earnings-beat-113418264.html ; Investing.com | https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/kla-q4-fy2026-slides-record-revenue-but-shares-fall-on-margin-mix-93CH-4818307 ; ChartMill | https://www.chartmill.com/news/KLAC/Chartmill-51870-KLA-Corp-NASDAQKLAC-Quality-Stock-Delivers-Earnings-Beat-Amid-Sector-Rout
**Related vault notes:** [[Ventures]]

Following up yesterday's open item: Corning posted Q2 core EPS of $0.78 (up 30% YoY, beating the $0.76 consensus) on core sales of $4.74bn (+17% YoY, beating $4.63bn consensus) — a clean beat on both lines — yet shares slid 14% in premarket trading on disappointing guidance. KLA reported record fiscal Q4 revenue of $3.66bn with non-GAAP EPS of $1.05 versus $1.0163 consensus, also a beat, and also guided Q1 revenue above Wall Street expectations — yet shares fell 6.18% in the regular session and another 7.74% after hours on margin-mix concerns.

The pattern across both names is the actual story: two genuine beats, two guidance-driven sell-offs. That is a market pricing AI-capex equipment names for perfection and punishing anything short of it, which is the same dynamic likely to govern tonight's Microsoft and Meta reaction (Story 3) regardless of headline numbers.

WTI jumped as much as 4.4% to $82.73/bbl and Brent gained to $87.34/bbl late yesterday after CENTCOM confirmed it intercepted an Iranian "attempted surprise attack" on US forces in the Middle East — a sharp reversal of the prior easing on Oman-Iran Hormuz diplomacy. As of today's Al Jazeera print, Brent had pulled back to $86.30/bbl, suggesting the acute spike is fading but the risk premium has not fully unwound. Full detail and the diplomatic context sit in [[Geopolitics]]; flagged here purely as the macro backdrop against which today's Fed decision and equity moves are happening.

This is the same dynamic as yesterday's Aramco/Abqaiq story: headline-driven oil volatility that matters directly for the risk premium the comparison network and property/mortgage climate price in, layered on top of a Fed that must now weigh energy-driven inflation risk in today's decision.
