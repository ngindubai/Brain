## Story 1: Crypto's best weekly run in years carries through the weekend

**Source:** Sunday Guardian Live | https://sundayguardianlive.com/business/bitcoin-price-today-22-august-why-is-bitcoin-up-today-btc-surges-above-77000-in-biggest-weekly-rally-in-3-years-check-latest-prices-in-usd-inr-gbp-euro-jpy-266930/ and CoinGabbar | https://www.coingabbar.com/en/price-prediction/why-are-bitcoin-ethereum-xrp-cardano-and-solana-prices-up-today and Coinpaper | https://coinpaper.com/34545/ethereum-price-today-eth-surges-to-2430-as-etf-inflows-reach-221m

Bitcoin is trading around $77,400–$77,500 today, having touched an intraday high near $79,500 on Friday before giving back a slice of the move. The week's gain sits at roughly 22–23%, which several outlets are independently calling Bitcoin's strongest five-day run in years. The follow-through is broad rather than Bitcoin-only: Ethereum is holding above $2,400 after breaking that level for the first time in about four months, XRP is up 19.6% over 24 hours to $1.42, and Solana is up 5% to $91.64. Total crypto market capitalisation has pushed back above $2.5 trillion, a three-month high.

The drivers are the same three that built through this week and are still doing the work today: the Treasury's decision to at least double its long-dated bond buybacks (which pulled yields and the dollar down and pushed risk appetite up), roughly $2.5bn in forced short-position liquidations that amplified the move mechanically, and continued institutional demand — spot Bitcoin ETFs pulled in over $600m on their best single day this week and spot Ethereum ETFs logged their strongest session since October 2025, with combined four-day inflows above $512m and assets under management at $13.58bn, the highest since May.

The regulatory tailwind cited everywhere in this rally's coverage is softer than the price action suggests. President Trump has been pushing Congress to pass the CLARITY Act, and that messaging is clearly part of the sentiment behind the rally, but the bill itself is stalled in the Senate over partisan ethics objections, a cloture motion filed 8 August still hasn't produced a floor vote, and the Senate isn't expected to take it up again until after its September recess. The rally is being bought on the promise of the bill, not its passage — worth remembering if the Senate calendar slips again, because that's a sentiment prop with nothing under it yet.

There's a second thread worth flagging without over-weighting it: multiple pieces this month have been arguing that AI stocks, not crypto, have become the market's preferred speculative outlet — semiconductor volatility (SOXX 60-day realised vol around 70%) now running well above Bitcoin's (around 30%), a genuine reversal of the historical pattern. That argument predates today and isn't new, but it's the right lens for reading this weekend's crypto strength: money is rotating between two systemically important behaviour, not settling permanently in either. It's a useful reminder for anyone judging market risk appetite off either asset class alone.
