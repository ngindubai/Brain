## Story 1: Reddit joins the S&P 500, forcing an estimated $2.9bn of passive buying

**Source:** Yahoo Finance | https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/article/reddit-stock-surges-after-sp-500-inclusion--heres-the-platforms-next-big-opportunity-093635663.html
**Related vault notes:** [[Priorities]]

Reddit (RDDT) entered the S&P 500 ahead of today's open, replacing AvalonBay Communities (which is being absorbed by Equity Residential). Shares jumped over 15% in early trading, on top of an 11%+ pop last week when the addition was first announced. J.P. Morgan estimates index funds need to buy roughly 16.7 million shares — about $2.9bn at last week's price — purely as mechanical rebalancing flow, independent of Reddit's fundamentals. It's only the second pure-play social platform in the index after Meta, and shares are up roughly 424% since the 2024 IPO.

This is a clean, dateable index-inclusion trade rather than a signal about the broader AI/tech tape — worth flagging as a technical event with a known settlement date, not as a read on sentiment. Watch tomorrow for whether the pop holds or mean-reverts once the forced buying clears.

## Story 2: Home Depot reports Q2 under an interim CEO, with tariff pass-through the real question

**Source:** TIKR | https://www.tikr.com/blog/home-depot-reports-q2-2026-earnings-on-august-18-can-it-finally-break-out
**Related vault notes:** [[Priorities]]

Home Depot reported fiscal Q2 before today's open, with the Street looking for adjusted EPS around $4.71-4.73 on roughly $47-47.5bn revenue against falling store traffic and flat-to-2% comp guidance. Two complications sharpen the read beyond the headline number: the report lands under an interim office of the CEO after Ted Decker's medical leave (announced 12 August), and new Section 338 duties of 50% on a range of Canadian goods take effect tomorrow, 19 August — one day after the print. Forward commentary on tariff pass-through matters more here than the quarter itself. As of filing (Dubai evening), the actual print's market reaction had not fully settled into a clean today-dated read — worth checking tomorrow's tape for the market's verdict rather than assuming a direction now.

## Story 3: Toll Brothers reports Q3 after the close, guiding into a double-digit revenue decline

**Source:** Alphastreet | https://news.alphastreet.com/toll-brothers-q3-2026-earnings-preview-august-19-street-expects-2-93-eps/amp/
**Related vault notes:** [[Priorities]]

Toll Brothers reports fiscal Q3 after today's close, with consensus at roughly $2.93 EPS on $2.62bn revenue — a projected 22%+ EPS decline and ~12% revenue decline year-on-year, with deliveries guided down to 2,600-2,700 units from 2,959 a year ago. Paired with Home Depot's soft comp guide (Story 2), a double-digit revenue decline at a builder is the clearest read available today on whether the 10-year yield sitting near cycle highs (see Market signals) is starting to bite discretionary housing spend. Genuinely useful leading-indicator territory for anyone tracking US consumer/rate-sensitivity ahead of the Fed's next move, though the actual print lands too late in the US day to have a settled market reaction at filing time.

## Story 4: Baidu reports Q2 — a real-money test of China's AI cloud monetisation story

**Source:** StockTitan | https://www.stocktitan.net/news/BIDU/baidu-to-report-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-on-august-18-61z0wxxelw4m.html
**Related vault notes:** [[Comparison Network]], [[Priorities]]

Baidu reported before today's US open (8pm Beijing time call). Street consensus was $1.46 EPS on $4.74bn revenue, but the setup going in was soft: five downward EPS revisions and ten downward revenue revisions ahead of the print. The number that matters is AI Cloud Infra growth — it ran 79% YoY in Q1 (RMB 8.8bn), with Baidu's AI-powered core business already 52% of general business revenue that quarter and ERNIE 5.1 topping Chinese-language leaderboards. This is the closest thing today has to a genuine Chinese-AI-stocks story from the sweep list: a direct data point on whether China's AI infrastructure spend is converting to cloud revenue at a comparable pace to US hyperscalers, at a moment when some analysts have been rotating Chinese AI-adjacent capital toward industrial automation/robotics names rather than core internet platforms. The actual Q2 figures were not yet indexed anywhere at filing time (Dubai evening) — worth a direct follow-up tomorrow rather than guessing at the number now.
