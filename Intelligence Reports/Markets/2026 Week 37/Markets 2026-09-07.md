## Story 3: Nvidia-Hugging Face deal confirmed as a full HSR/EU antitrust filing, not a Groq-style structured workaround

**Source:** The Register | https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/03/hugging-face-is-too-important-to-fall-into-nvidias-hands/5294363 and SEC 8-K filing | https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000078/nvda-20260902.htm

Nvidia's SEC 8-K confirms the $12.9 billion Hugging Face acquisition (roughly $11.9bn to shareholders plus ~$1bn in employee retention equity) is structured as a direct acquisition, meaning it triggers a full Hart-Scott-Rodino review in the US and a merger review in the EU — unlike Nvidia's ~$20bn Groq transaction, which was structured to avoid a full antitrust filing. Nvidia executives are framing the deal publicly as a "deconcentration platform" that keeps the Hugging Face ecosystem open and hardware-agnostic, an argument clearly aimed at regulators. Expected close is H1 2027.

This isn't new information dated today, but it's a genuinely different framing from what this report carried on the deal's signing (3 Sept): the market had been treating this as effectively done, and the confirmed HSR/EU filing requirement means it now has a real regulatory clock attached, not a formality. Worth tracking as the closest thing to an antitrust test case for AI-infrastructure consolidation this year.
