## Story 1: Bank of Japan meets today for an expected hike to 1.25% — no confirmed outcome by filing time

**Source:** FXStreet | https://www.fxstreet.com/news/125-why-the-bank-of-japan-hike-is-a-done-deal-and-the-japanese-yen-now-hinges-on-uedas-guidance-202609171451 | Investing.com | https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/boj-preview-september-25-bps-hike-expected-with-hawkish-outlook-4904796

The BoJ's two-day policy meeting concludes today, with the decision statement due around midday Tokyo time and Governor Ueda's press conference at 3:30pm JST (roughly 11:30am UAE time). Markets are pricing a 25bp hike to 1.25% as close to certain — FXStreet's Thursday preview called it "a done deal," with the live question being how hawkish Ueda's forward guidance is on the pace of further tightening, given money markets are pricing roughly 90bp of cumulative hikes over the next 12 months. This would be Japan's most tightly spaced tightening cycle in decades, landing three months after the last move and in the same week the Fed itself hiked. A genuine dual-tightening moment across the world's largest and third-largest economies is unusual and worth tracking for its effect on the yen carry trade that has funded a chunk of this year's global risk-asset positioning. Caveat directly: at filing time, no clean, single-sourced confirmation of the actual decision or Ueda's comments had turned up in available reporting — search results returned a mix of Thursday's previews and what appear to be templated/aggregated summaries with inconsistent numbers (one snippet cited a 0.5% starting rate, which contradicts confirmed reporting of a 1% rate since June). Treat the 1.25% hike as highly likely but not yet confirmed; first check tomorrow.

## Story 2: Triple witching lands on top of the BoJ decision — a genuine options/futures event, not just a calendar note

**Source:** Trading Economics (via search) | https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/stock-market

Today is the third-Friday quarterly triple witching session — simultaneous expiry of stock options, index options and index futures — landing on the same day as the BoJ decision above. Triple witching mechanically inflates trading volume in the settlement window and can distort intraday price moves in ways that don't reflect a change in underlying fundamentals, which matters directly for anyone reading today's headline index moves at face value. The S&P 500 closed Thursday (17 September) at 7,596, up 0.59% on the day and 14.5% higher than a year ago, so the tape was already constructive heading into today's dual event. No confirmed closing data for today's session was available at filing time; today's move should be read with the witching effect discounted rather than as a clean signal either way.

## Story 4: Chinese AI stock selloff deepens as Anthropic and other US developers push Washington to restrict Chinese access to frontier models

**Source:** Bloomberg | https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/china-s-ai-selloff-faces-risk-from-us-push-for-model-curbs

Z.AI and MiniMax have both fallen more than 30% this month, wiping a combined $33 billion in market value, on fierce competition and cash-burn concerns. The new element today is regulatory: Anthropic and other US AI developers are lobbying policymakers to restrict Chinese labs' access to frontier models, accusing some Chinese rivals of using American models without authorisation to train their own through distillation. Chinese state media (China Daily) has already pushed back, framing the US push as a "self-serving" effort by American executives to blunt Chinese competition now that hardware export controls have failed to stop competitive Chinese models emerging. This lands one week before the Bessent-led US-China AI dialogue folds into the broader economic track ahead of Xi's Washington visit (see Geopolitics report), so the distillation dispute is now a live input into that negotiation, not just a market story. Watch whether Beijing raises it explicitly in next week's talks.

## Story 5: CME Group confirms 5 October launch and contract specs for GPU compute futures

**Source:** CME Group | https://www.cmegroup.com/media-room/press-releases/2026/8/11/cme_group_and_silicondatatolaunchcomputefuturesonoctober5tounloc.html

This thread has sat parked in this brief since the initial 11 August announcement. Confirmed detail now available: two contracts — the Silicon Data H100 Rental Index Futures and the Silicon Data B200 Rental Index Futures — will list on 5 October, pending regulatory review, each representing a month's rental cost for an Nvidia H100 or Blackwell B200 respectively. They trade on NYMEX under CME rules, clear through standard CME systems, and list monthly out to 36 months on Globex under standard energy-product trading hours. This is the first tradeable hedge against AI compute cost specifically, which is the direct research input for the GPU compute venture: a live futures curve from 5 October gives a genuine forward-looking price signal on GPU rental costs rather than relying on vendor list prices or anecdote.
