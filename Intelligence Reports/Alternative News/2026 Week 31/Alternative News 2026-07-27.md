**Source:** The Intercept | https://theintercept.com/2026/07/26/cuba-marco-rubio-state-department-report/
**Author:** Noah Hurowitz | **Published:** 26 July 2026, 06:00 ET | **Read time:** ~5 min

Per the granularity rule this is not written up as today's story, since it carries yesterday's date, but it did not appear in the 26 July report (which recorded The Intercept's feed as current only through 25 July at the time of that day's fetch) and is worth logging in full now rather than dropping it silently.

The Intercept reports that a 100-page State Department document, produced under Secretary of State Marco Rubio, casts Cuba as the "capital of 21st century communism" and alleges the country runs a wide-ranging influence operation behind Black Lives Matter protests, anti-ICE activism, antifascist organising and recruitment on American university campuses. The report names domestic US groups, including the Democratic Socialists of America, Code Pink and the National Lawyers Guild, as accomplices of Cuban intelligence. Rep. Mark Pocan (D-Wis.) called it "the stupidest, most fantastical, most biased thing" produced by any government agency, and Rep. Delia Ramirez (D-Ill.) said it was "intentionally designed to instill fear of the Cuban people and to justify a military attack on Cuba."

The story's significance is twofold: it reframes a US foreign-policy document as also functioning as a domestic surveillance and protest-suppression instrument, tying an external adversary narrative to groups already exercising First Amendment rights at home, and it comes from Congressional Democrats reading a document that would otherwise get little independent scrutiny.
